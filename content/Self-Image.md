Alias: Self-Worth, Self-Recognition

[[Yoga]] means being feeling worthy - that you have ripened.

How do you feel worthy? Start measuring your impact. Accurately measuring your impact is a **skill**. The skill of knowing. Then you'll know where you stand. Always know where you stand. A lot of people don't know where they stand in the world. Due to fear of rejection.  If you get the **skill** of handling rejection without being hurt, then you can measure your impact.
