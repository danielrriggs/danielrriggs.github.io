Sanskrit for posture. Really means "seat". Yoga poses. The goal of Asanas is to purify the body & prepare it for meditation (condition it).

Asana as defined by Patanjali is a steady and comfortable sitting position. The purpose of Asana in [[Patanjali Yoga]] is to balance the different nerve impulses, feelings of pain and pleasure, heat and cold and all other sensations.

The third step in the [[Eight Limbs of Raja Yoga]].

Asanas are about learning to seat yourself in various places of consciousness - There are four states of [[Consciousness]] & sixteen [[Adhara]] (which give you sixty-four asanas).

If you master these sixty-four asanas and you learn to seat yourself in various places of consciousness, then you have a great advantage because you know how to see the full picture, the big picture, the all picture.
