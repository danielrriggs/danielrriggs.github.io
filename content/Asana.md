Alias: asanas

###### Definition 1
Asana really means "seat".

How is physicality connected to [[Yoga]]? Your posture and your body language is very important for how you feel. That's the the value of physical [[Yoga]]. If it is making you feel good, then it's successful asana.

Asana is just how you sit. Asana means **body language**, not posture. It's how you present yourself and show up in the world.

###### Definition 2
Sanskrit for posture. Yoga poses. The goal of Asanas is to purify the body & prepare it for meditation (condition it).

Asana as defined by Patanjali is a steady and comfortable sitting position. The purpose of Asana in [[Patanjali Yoga]] is to balance the different nerve impulses, feelings of pain and pleasure, heat and cold and all other sensations.

The third step in the [[Eight Limbs of Raja Yoga]].

###### Commentary
Asanas are about learning to seat yourself in various places of consciousness - There are four states of [[Consciousness]] & sixteen [[Adhara]]s (which give you sixty-four asanas).

If you master these sixty-four asanas and you learn to seat yourself in various places of consciousness, then you have a great advantage because you know how to see the full picture, the big picture, the all picture.
