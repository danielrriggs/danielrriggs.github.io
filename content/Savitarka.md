In [[Meditation]], there are numerous gross level objects on which one can concentrate for meditation, including physical objects or their mental forms, attitudes, and a variety of thought processes.

- [[Chakras]]
- Visualizations
- [[Mantra]]
- Friendliness
- Compassion
- [[Prana|Breath]]
- Benevolence
- Acceptance
- Purity of mind
- Luminosity
- Thought streams
- Energy
- Subtle senses
- Anything pleasing
