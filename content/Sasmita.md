Beyond the gross ([[Savitarka]]), subtle ([[Savichara]]), and bliss ([[Sananda]]) levels, there is meditation on I-ness itself. This is not an ego level, along with the many [[Personality]] traits, wants, wishes, images, or forms. Rather, it is [[Meditation]] on individuality itself, untainted by all of those more surface level experiences. Though very deep, it has a simplicity in that it is a single I-ness, rather than a diversity of choices of [[gross]] or [[subtle]] objects.
