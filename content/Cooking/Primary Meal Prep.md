My implementation of the [[Cooking/Vertical Diet|Vertical Diet]]'s Monster Mash.

- **Chuck Roast** - ~11ish lbs (2x Costco)
	- Salt & pepper. Sear both sides for 2 minutes on high heat (in a little beef tallow). Add 2 containers of Beef Broth to pressure cooker (instant pot). Cook on high for 54 minutes.
		- Ingredients: Salt, pepper, beef tallow, beef broth.
- **Ground Beef** - ~6.5 lbs (1x Costco)
	- Ingredients:
		- [[Spice Mix#Ground Beef Seasoning]]
- **Spinach** - 8lbs
	- Cooked on stove with Wok.
	- Ingredients:
		- [[Spice Mix#General Spice Mix]]
- **Bell Pepper** - 12 (2x Costco Bags)
	- Cooked on stove with Wok.
		- [[Spice Mix#General Spice Mix]]
- **Eggs** - 5 dozen (1x Costco box)
	- Ingredients: Butter, salt, pepper
- **Rice** - ~10 cups
	- Cooked in rice cooker.

[[Spice Mix]] is prepped in mason jars.
