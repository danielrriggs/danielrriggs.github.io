Cut into wedges
Soak wedges in ice-water for 30 min (makes extra crispy)

Drain. Wipe wedges. You can spread them on a kitchen town for an hour to get rid of excess moisture.

In a mixing bowl, add:
- 1 tsp olive oil
- Salt (to taste)
- Pepper (to taste)
- 1/2 tsp rosemary
- 1/2 tsp oregano

Mix in the potatoes.

Air fry @ 390 for 15 min. Toss Wedges. Air fry 1-2 min until brown & crisp. Every air fryer is different; adjust accordingly.
