There are 16 phases of the moon. The Sun is treated as just one celestial object because it's pretty much the same all the time, but the moon gets lit differently and it changes its shape throughout the 16 phases which have completely different effects.

The yogis have identified 16 points in your body called [[Adhara]]s.

Each of the moon phases is optimal for something and each of the [[Adhara]]s is associated with one moon phase. This is a very advanced, very deep practice which isn't relevant for a beginner.
