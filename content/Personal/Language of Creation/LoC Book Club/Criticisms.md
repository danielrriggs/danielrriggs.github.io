Plato - Being and Becoming as the basis of all things. Emerson, who called it Motion and Rest. Emerson called these, "the first and second secret of nature." Pageau calls them Time and Space.

These are, indeed, the basis of all creation and therefore a basic part of our nature. Our Sympathetic and Parasympathetic Nervous Systems, for example, work with these principles.

Pageau says that "spacial reality, or 'dry-land' is the very embodiment of righteousness and truth in this cosmology" he is taking things too far.

This would make the flood evil, instead of God's way of washing away evil from the earth. Within this cosmology Water was the equivalent of Time, an agent to bring about change. Change can seem evil, if you like things the way they are. It can seem good, if you hate the way things are. It is all a matter of ones perspective at the time.
