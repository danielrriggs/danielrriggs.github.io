[[Bharata]] wrote a book which is more important than Yoga Sutra for the yogis.

Natyashatra is the method by which theatrical performances are to be performed and created.

Natya Shastra, written by Bharata, is more important than [[Yoga Sutra]] for the yogis because it helps you to build your proper [[Itihasa]] which is crucial to build your [[Swamurthi]]. When you have a divine [[Itihasa]], you're attached to a lineage, then your [[Swamurthi]] also becomes divine.
