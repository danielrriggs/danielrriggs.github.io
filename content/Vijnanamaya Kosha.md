(Vig-yaan-nuh-may-uh Ko-sh-uh)

The intellectual body. Layer 4 in [[Panchakosha Theory]]. Our faculty for analysis. Our ability to have thoughts & critical thinking. Does not include emotions. Emotions & thoughts are different things.

This is where we have our [[Ego|identity]] / [[Ego]] / sense of [[Nature of Self|self]].

The Vijnanamaya Kosha is composed of:
- [[Buddhi]]
- [[Ego|ahamkara]]

Vijnanamaya kosha is the sheath in which we make the leap from egocentricity — a sense of self that holds us in time and space — to pure consciousness. The [[Buddhi]] analyzes and determines the true nature of all objects. [[Ego|Ahamkara]] is the self-asserting principle and works with the five organs of knowledge. [[Viveka]], or discrimination and decision-making are the workings of the intellectual sheath. The cultivation of [[Vairagya|Detachment]] is how we harmonize this sheath. This is only possible once we have harmonized the first three sheaths. [[Vairagya|Detachment]] requires us to be able to observe while remaining neutral on an emotional level.

The head of the Vijnanamaya Kosha is [[Sraddha|Shraddha]].
