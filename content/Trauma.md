Alias: traumatic, traumatized, traumatizing

Most people have some trauma. It can manifest as all kinds of other diagnoses, [[ADHD]], chronic [[depression]], panic attacks, etc - can have their origin in trauma.

Medicine is the practice of fixing things that are malfunctioning. Trauma is different. Trauma is not a pathology. It's an adaptation. Trauma is your body, brain, and mind's attempt to survive a negative experience. Then we run into trouble because what allows us to survive in one situation create problems down the line.

Examples:
- [[Procrastination]]
- Lack of [[Motivation]]
- Perfectionism
- Being unable to plan for the future and move consistently towards a goal
- Not knowing who you are
- Difficulty in my relationships
- Inability to take risks
- Addictions; self medicating some storm on the inside

These kinds of experiences are exceedingly common. Research backs this up - studies on ACEs (adverse childhood experiences).
Examples:
- Parents getting divorced
- Getting bulled growing up
- Moving around a lot growing up
The more of these kinds of experiences that you have, the more likely you are to have difficulties in all of the domains that we discussed. 60% of people have at least one adverse childhood experience. Everyone goes through particularly hard times at some point in their life, whether it be bullying or a breakup. The young brain tries to survive a circumstance and learns a lesson that creates problems in the future.

Understand the process of learning and adaptation happens. Rewire so we can becomes the person we want to be.

Research on trauma shows that these adaptations can happen to children or adults.
