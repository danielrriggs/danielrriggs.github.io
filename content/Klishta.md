A coloring ([[Rasa]]? [[Raga]] & [[Dvesha]]?) that the [[Mind]] attaches to a stimulus.

A Klishta is more primitive than an [[Attachment]] (the [[Mind]]'s projection of the importance of something).

Let's say I order a coffee, but when I receive it, I realize it is not what I ordered. I like one drink, and I dislike another drink. The world is what it is, but your [[Mind]] attaches negativity or positivity to objects. It's in the [[Mind]], not the object itself.

[[Dukkha|Suffering]] comes from the coloring of my mind.

The more insidious thing is that, as we develop more and more klishtas, our sense of [[Perception]] deviates more and more from [[Reality]].

People who are [[Trauma|traumatized]] [[Attachment|attach]] all kinds of [[Meaning]]s to simple stimuli.
	Ex: I texted someone and they didn't text back - *"This means they don't like me; I'm annoying them; they're hanging out with someone else, etc."* The real problem isn't the world out there, it's the flavoring their [[Mind]] adds to the experience of their life.

The process of uncoloring a klishta primarily happens with observation.
