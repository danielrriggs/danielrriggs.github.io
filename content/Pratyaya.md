An object or symbol. When we have a mental experience of an object. They can be either [[gross]] or [[subtle]]. We can have a pratyaya with or without the [[Indriya|Indriya]]s coming in contact with an object.

[[Pratyaya]] depends on the three [[Gunas]]; there are [[Sattva|Sattwic]], [[Rajas|Rajasic]] and [[Tamas|Tamasic]] [[Pratyaya]].

Example [[Pratyaya]]s used for [[Samadhi#Sabeeja Samadhi]]:
- a [[Chakras]]
- a lotus
- a sound
- a mudra
- [[Pranayama]]
- a smell
- a sensation in [[Muladhara Chakra|Mooladhara]]
- a light in [[Bhrumadhya]]
- one’s own guru
