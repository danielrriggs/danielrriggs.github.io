"seal", "mark", or "gesture". A symbolic or ritual gesture or pose.

"Attitude" in English. Adopting a certain posture or direction with our meditative practice. Enhances the potency of the practice.

- Jnana Mudra - hand looks like 'Ok'.
- Nasika Mudra
	- Used in [[Nadi Shuddhi]]
- Kechari Mudra - bottom of tongue against roof of mouth into nasal cavity.
	- Has esoteric / spiritual significance. Helps us conquer the [[Abhinivesha|fear of death]].
