 A system that works on your body and mind to create immortality.

But, if you've got a really sucky life, then what's the use of making immortality? Yoga vayama takes care of that, too.

As you practice it, it increases your intellect, your emotional capacities, and your status. 

Yoga vayama is a mechanism for grace, health, status, [[Mental Health]], and emotional freedom.

It's a system of movement.

###### Status
Anyone can wear clothes and fake status, but a person who's genuinely of a higher status will have a different body language. Status is shown with body language. Confidence. A bearing of knowing your body - of relaxation and being in control. You have no tension. 

You can't assign yourself a status. Other people have to accept it, and that's when you actually have status.

The practice of Yoga Vyayama improves your own status in your mind, and eventually other people's appreciation of you and your status increases. Then cooperation will increase, which will create alignment with the world.

if somebody's going to give you something, you're in a receptive mode. When you're going to give something, you're going to give it when someone is in a receptive mode. You develop a sense of timing, and you develop a sense of grace in the world.

###### Keep Your Shit Together
We need people who are keeping their shit together. Everybody is losing their shit when they get COVID and other stuff. People don't know how to handle death. A bunch of dominos will fall. People will die, lose their jobs, economies will suffer, relationships will break down. People who are practicing yoga vayama will be ready for it. They've got the body language and the presence, the rootedness, the vision, the relaxation, and the cheerfulness to handle whatever life throws at them. It's a superpower. It's called being a [[Yogis|Yogi]]. It all begins with the body.

**How's it different from working out in the gym?**
According to the gym, there is only one body, Annamaya Kosha. That's it. Nothing about your thoughts, destiny, luck, life force.

The physical body is one-fifth of a human being according to ... one of the Upanishads (I forget... there are 108).

The life force body needs to be controlled with quality of movement and breath.
The mental body needs to be controlled by movement again. Wherever your body is limited, you're also meeting the limitations of the mental body. But doing it in a systematic way without getting injuries.

Yoga Vyayama has guidance about nutrition.
There's meditation embedded in Yoga Vyayama - 16 meditations in which we learn to control the mind.

At the most, the Chinese will say focus on your Uddiyana. Dantain, they call it. Focus on the Dantain. And feel that you're connected to the earth. And then feel like your spine is being pulled up by something. That's the maximum instruction I got from any Chinese instructor. Yoga Vyayama has very specific detailed meditations that make changes in your mental body.

Then there is the [[Vijnanamaya Kosha]] where the logic is stored, where justice is stored, all our justice system. We learn how to empty all that in Yoga Vyayama. Get rid of it.

Then there's the [[Anandamaya Kosha]] where all of the pain is stored. By converting that pain, when we remove the justice system, the Anandamaya Kosha's pain converts into joy.


Change in the physical level is the result of the other four levels. [[Panchakosha Theory|Panchakosha]] considers all the body systems to be one. If there's a problem, you don't treat a specific thing, or sub-system. You treat the whole body. Dividing the body into systems is just for convenience. The body sees itself as one thing.

As you're practicing Yoga Veyama, you understand you have deeper and bigger ambitions to improve your status in the world.

There's no way that you practice Yoga Veyama for one year and you don't become a yogi. You'll fall in alignment. You'll drop your stupid system of justice and you'll say, I deserve the best here.
