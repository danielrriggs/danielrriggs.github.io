The goals of trauma treatment:
1) Safety
A lot of people who struggle with [[Trauma]] disorders are fundamentally in unsafe lives. This can be a desire for suicide or self-harm or where we're in relationships or in living circumstances where we are in danger of abuse or further [[Trauma]].

Sometimes this requires some pretty drastic measures including in the case of people who are acutely suicidal or homicidal or dissociative and unable to take care of themselves - things like hospitalization.

2) Change Physiology
Parts of my body that feel like we are in danger even though we're not, so we need to recalibrate that physiology to a more baseline level.

3) Develop healthy relationship with emotions
In the case of [[Trauma]], emotions become scary. Remember that emotions are sources of information and sources of [[Motivation]]. When emotions become overwhelming and we can't integrate them into the other parts of our life in a healthy way, this becomes problematic.

4) Reshape cognition
We want to change the way we think. Oftentimes as a consequence of our traumatic experiences, the way we see and think about the world is unhealthy. It creates a pessimistic view where we're always falling short and we're beating ourselves up and everyone else is better. We want to be very careful about recrafting our sense of [[Ego|identity]]. Generally speaking, after we've experienced [[Trauma]], we have a very messed up sense of [[Ego|identity]]. Our sense of [[Self-Image|Self-Esteem]] can be very damaged.

We'll use tools like narrative to recreate a sense of who we are in life. We know that narrative and creating a story about your life is very useful in creating a healthy sense of [[Ego|identity]].

5) Reengage with Joy in life
The experience of a lot of people who have been traumatized is that life is no longer fun. Once we've experienced [[Trauma]], life is no longer about joy, it's about survival. We distrust the world. "How do I get through the day? How do I get through the next day of work? How do I get through this job interview?"

6) Healthy Relationships
We need to connect with other people in a healthy way.

**Specifics of Trauma Treatment:**
1) Deal with Intrusive Thoughts
When someone has something like [[Post Traumatic Stress Disorder|PTSD]], they don't "control" what enters their mind, what they dream about, or how their mind interprets signals. For example, if I call someone I care about and they don't answer the phone, it can triggers fears of abandonment and I can catastrophize. Even though I don't want to be thinking about this stuff, I can't control it. Goals of treatment include changing our relationship with intrusive thoughts and protecting us against those kinds of intrusions.
2) Reduce Hyperarousal of Nervous System

3) Correct Previous Adaptations
In the process of survival, we will develop a lot of very unhealthy behaviors. The most common being something like [[Addiction]]. To manage my torrent of negative emotions, I turn to substances to numb me out. A lot of the challenges that people with trauma disorders face are not just the trauma disorder itself, but the **behaviors** that they engage in to protect themselves from the trauma disorder, which can cause further problems.

Another good example of this is self-harm behaviors. Things like cutting or burning are very specific and accomplish something that is almost meditative in nature. I'm a fan of the theory that self-harm behaviors are an emotional regulation technique.

Cutting is superficial. It's not something that damages our tissue. The superficial area of our skin is the area where we have the most sensitive pain receptors. They're inducing the most pain while minimizing the damage they're doing to their body. Why are they trying to maximize pain and minimize damage? They're more likely to self-harm when they are feeling emotionally overwhelmed or dysregulated. Pain clears those thoughts from the mind. If I'm feeling abandoned, pathetic, scared, suicidal, I can engage in self-harm behaviors to wipe all those thoughts out of my mind.

4) Reduce avoidance behaviors
Remember that in [[Post Traumatic Stress Disorder|PTSD]], we get triggered, so we craft a life in avoidance of the [[Trauma]]. But, this cuts off different parts of life. We aren't able to engage in relationships, etc.

Modalities for [[Trauma]] treatment
- [[Psychotherapy]]
	- There are different types
		- Trauma Processing Therapy
		- Trauma Focused CBT
	- Things less well known / we aren't sure how good they are:
		- Psychodynamic Therapy
		- Psychoanalysis - this is originally what [[Sigmund Freud|Freud]] did, and he had at least some degree of success.
- Others that can be effective:
	- [[Exposure Therapy]]
	- [[Dialectical Behavioral Therapy|DBT]]

**Medications**
In [[Bipolar Disorder]], we have mood stabilizers. We alternate between mania / hypomania & [[Depression]].

But, in the case of [[Trauma]], we don't have medication to fix all of the adaptations in your system. We'll use medication for symptomatic management.

For example, if a patient can't sleep because they have nightmares, we can sedate them & suppress nightmares.

We can use medications to stop the physiologic [[Sympathetic Nervous System|SNS]] response (like adrenaline response).

There really aren't great medications that are very effective at treating [[Trauma]] disorders.
