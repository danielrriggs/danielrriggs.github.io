A state in which we move beyond the [[Samadhi#Three streams of awareness]] that define lower samadhi.

In [[Asamprajnata]] samadhi, there is no awareness of any [[Pratyaya]]. This is called [[Virama]] [[Pratyaya]], but the mere absence of awareness of a [[Pratyaya]] does not mean asamprajnata.

This state of [[Laya]] is not free from [[Samskara]]s. The consciousness is not static there, although there is absence of the symbol. Even during the [[Laya]] state, there is an underground dynamism called [[Samskara]]. When the [[Samskara]] is completely finished, the [[Consciousness]] is completely dissolved; that state is [[Samadhi#Nirbeeja Samadhi]] because there is no need of consciousness.

In Asamprajnata there are two distinct characteristics: 
- Dropping of the [[Pratyaya]]
- The presence of [[Samskara]]

The state of asamprajnata is similar to [[Nirodha]]. From it, one may either ascend to a deeper state of [[Consciousness]] or one may revert back to a grosser state.
