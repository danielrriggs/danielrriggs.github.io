A form of [[Cognitive Behavioral Therapy|CBT]] designed to teach people how to live in the moment, develop healthy ways to cope with stress, regulate their emotions, and improve their relationships with others.

It's like [[Cognitive Behavioral Therapy|CBT]] with a heavy chunk of [[Mindfulness]] or [[Meditation]] thrown in.

DBT was originally studied in [[Borderline Personality Disorder|BPD]] and is effective at treating it. Some of the skills training in DBT - [[Grounding Meditation Technique|grounding techniques]], [[Mindfulness|mindfulness]] skills - are used as coping mechanisms.
