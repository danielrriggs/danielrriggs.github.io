Two sanskrit words na iti (thus, so) which comes together to form the word neti.

"not this, not that"

Who are you? When people ask, we say a profession or a relationship (son / daughter of ...). We create fake identities. So you can never be known. Like a camera can't take a picture of itself. Your [[Mind]] is like a camera and whatever it can picture is definitely not it.

If you can think you're something, you're not that.

When you understand this deep truth, it's so relieving that you don't have to own any of the praise and none of the blame that has been thrown on you in your life. Even praise is a burden because people expect you to live up to a particular behavior.

it's very important to remember that you're not any of the things that you were, or people say you were.

Neti Neti is a systematic way of disentangling, essentially, the sense of identity, of consciousness, from the conglomerate of, soul, ([[Atman]]), which is nothing other than totality of awareness, is dressed in. The [[Atman|Soul]], is dressed in layers.
