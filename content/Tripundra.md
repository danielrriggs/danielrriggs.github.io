"Three marks".

The Tripuṇḍra, three horizontal lines on the forehead as well as other body parts are symbols during rites of passages, and for some a daily practice.

These lines represent Shiva’s threefold power of will (icchāśakti), knowledge (jñānaśakti), and action (kriyāśakti).

Before that triad of the **will**, **knowledge** and **action** comes [[Ananda|Bliss]].
