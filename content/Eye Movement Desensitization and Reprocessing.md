Alias: EMDR

Previously regarded as pseudoscience, but now has more evidence supporting it. Protocolizes a lot of what we try to do in terms of goals of [[Trauma]] treatment.

Someone is asked to recall and even verbalize a traumatic experience. While they are speaking about the traumatic experience, a therapist will ask them to focus on a finger or an object while the patient does the talking.

Normally, when we recall a trauma or we talk about a trauma, our attention is focused on the memory. The moment we start thinking about it, those memories and sensations fill our head. We can trigger something like a flashback. But by putting our attention away from the trauma and into some kind of stimulus, we short circuit this [[Trauma]] reaction. When people engage in EMDR, it allows them to re-access some of these traumatic experiences without triggering some of these traumatic responses.

We don't completely understand how this works. And, there are critics of EMDR. But a lot of people swear by it and there's some evidence for it.
