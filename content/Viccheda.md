Yoga's sadhana is mainly based on breath and on breath control ([[Pranayama]]). [[Pranayama]] is far from being a prerogative of [[Tantra|Tantrism]] alone. It is also found in classical yoga. [[Patanjali]] describes it as "the cutting off of the flow of inhalation and exhalation after assuming a steady and comfortable position." A real suspension of breath (**viccheda**) takes place only in the last stages of the yogic practice, which even in subsequent stages is referred to as [[Pranayama]].
