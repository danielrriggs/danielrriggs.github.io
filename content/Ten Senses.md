The subtle receptive senses:
1) Seeing
2) Smelling
3) Touching
4) Tasting
5) Hearing

The gross senses, or, the active senses:
1) The hands
2) Feet
3) Speech
4) The organs of elimination
5) The organs of generation

These ten senses are employed by your [[Mind]]. All of its input is filtered by these ten senses.
