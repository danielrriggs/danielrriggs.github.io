This practice also comes from the text of [[Vijnanabhairava Tantra]].

You're made of two things:
1) [[Desire]]s
2) [[Memory|Memories]]

Any sense of [[Nature of Self|self]] within you is either a desire or a memory.

**The Practice:**
We're going to pay attention to who we are, but we're going to discard anything that is a desire or anything that is born of a memory. Without these two things, we're looking for what is left.

You are not your desires or memories. You were you before you fulfilled your desires of today and had today's memories.

There's a final part which you can engage in if you want to, which is the reflection on a particular [[Mantra]]. This mantra is called a [[Mahavakya]], which means it is a "great sentence".

Utter "Aham Brahmasmi". Even without knowing what the Sanskrit means, as you utter it, you may develop a sense of what it means.
