Alias: Shraddha.

Sraddha gets translated as things like faith, confidence, belief.

Shraddha is made of two parts: shrat meaning ‘truth’, and dha meaning ‘to hold’'.

Thus, shraddha means ‘that which holds the truth’.

The word faith is perhaps not a correct translation of the word shraddha. Shraddha comes only after understanding the truth and arises from true experience. Shraddha can be had only after having a glimpse of truth.
It is, however, not the whole of truth, but only a glimpse; it is just the beginning of the path of [[Yoga]].
