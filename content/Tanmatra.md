_tan,_ means [[subtle]]; _matra_ means [[Panchabhuta|Element]] - subtle elements.
Another meaning of *tan* is mother, and *matra* also means matter — the mother of matter.

Tanmatras are the five objects of perception, made up of the five subtle elements.

According to this principle, each of the five senses (sound, touch, sight, taste and smell) corresponds with one or more of the five elements (ether, air, fire, water and earth).

According to [[Samkhya|Sankhya]] philosophy (a branch of Hindu philosophy), there are twenty-four principles of creation known as [[Tattwa|Tattva]]s. Tanmatras make up five of these [[Tattwa|Tattva]]s. They can be understood as the [[subtle]] root energy of all five elements, from which sensory perception arises.

###### The 5 Subtle Elements
The 5 subtle forms of the elements, or, the subtle modes of sensory content.
Also called **the 5 subtle senses**.
1. Sound - _[[Shabda]] Tanmatra_ – [[Akasha|Ether]] element, associated with sound.
2. Touch - _[[Sparsha]] Tanmatra_ – [[Vaayu|Air]] element, associated with sound and touch.
3. Form - _[[Rupa]] Tanmatra_ – [[Agni|Fire]] element, associated with sound, touch and sight.
4. Taste - _[[Rasa (Tanmatra)|Rasa]] Tanmatra_ – [[Jal|Water]] element, associated with sound, touch, sight and taste.
5. Smell - _[[Gandha]] Tanmatra_ – [[Prithvi|Earth]] element, associated with sound, touch, sight, taste and smell.
