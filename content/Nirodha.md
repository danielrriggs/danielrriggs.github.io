Alias: Nirodhah.

Blocking.

Control, not in the sense of suppression, but channeling or regulating.

Nirodhah is from the basic word [[Rodha]], which means an act of blocking. We have words developed from this root: 
- Rodha - an act of blocking
- [[Avarodha]] - obstruction
- Nirodha - blocking
- [[Virodha]] - Opposition
- [[Avarodha]] - obstruction
