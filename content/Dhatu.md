Alias: saptadhatu, Sapta Dhatus
(saptadhatu, means “seven dhatu.”)

“element,” “verb root”.

###### The Seven Dhatus:
1. Rasa – Plasma – Nourishment
2. Rakta – Blood – Circulation
3. Mamsa – Muscle – Movement
4. Medha – Fat – Lubrication
5. Asthi – Bone – Support
6. Majja – Bone marrow – Generation of blood cells
7. Shukra – Sexual hormones – Reproduction

The [[Dosha|tridoshas]] all regulate the dhatu. The seven dhatu draw energy from each other. Each dhatu can influence the health of the others. In [[Ayurveda]], the health of the body and mind is linked to the health conditions associated with these seven dhatu.
