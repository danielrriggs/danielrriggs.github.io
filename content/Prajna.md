The highest and purest form of [[Wisdom]].

The ultimate understanding of the true nature of existence and [[Reality]].

Derived from the roots *pra*, meaning “beginning” or “premium,” and *jna*, meaning “[[Consciousness]]” or “[[Jnana|understanding]].”

Prajna is a state of pure consciousness that transcends worldly concepts or belief systems that might impede perfect wisdom.

Unconscious.

###### Prajna vs Vijnana
Prajna is [[Wisdom]] or [[Jnana|understanding]]. There is a slight difference between Wisdom and Intelligence ([[Vijnana]]).

Prajna is having an inner, a deeply embedded unconscious understanding of things.

Prajna is a living understanding. A constantly modifying understanding. It's not from past data, it's from present circumstances that you have Prajna.
