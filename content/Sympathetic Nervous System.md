A division of the nervous system that functions to produce localized adjustments (such as sweating as a response to an increase in temperature) and reflex adjustments of the cardiovascular system.

When the SNS is active, heart rate can be elevated; blood pressure can be elevated. We route blood to parts of our brain like our danger & survival circuitry in our brain, as well as to our arms & legs. We pull blood away from things like our stomach & intestines.

The body wants to prioritize danger calculations & abilities for **fight**, or in the most severe cases, **freeze** or in the most severe cases, **freeze**. The freeze response is a hyperactive Sympathetic Nervous System response which comes at the end of fight or flight. An animal plays dead when it's caught. This level of freeze is so profound that, even if you damage the animal (poke it, bite it, cut it) its reflexes will be suppressed.

The SNS, when it's active, it affects the way that we **think**. We start to think in terms of very black and white. We start to make decisions quickly. We don't tend to think things through.
