Alias: Seed Body, Body of Ignorance, Body of Knowledge.

The unconscious.

The unmanifest realm of pure potentiality that we experience in states as deep-sleep and [[Nirvikalpa Samadhi]].

Contains the [[Anandamaya Kosha]].

The causal body is not the [[Atman]].

The causal body is the cause of our birth, rebirth, and ultimate liberation. The cause of birth and rebirth is ignorance. The only ingredient in the causal body is ignorance, or knowledge.
