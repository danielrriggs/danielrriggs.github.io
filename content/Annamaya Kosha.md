(Un-nuh-may-uh Koh-sh-uh)

Our physical body (form). Layer 1 in [[Panchakosha Theory]].

Created by the accumulation of food over our lifetime.

To balance this kosha, we need to fuel our body with healthy foods. Movement, including [[Asana]] and exercise, mudras, clean water and air, and healing modalities are tools that will bring balance to this kosha.

In [[Ayurveda]], the emphasis to balance this kosha is placed on diet. The element associated with this kosha is [[Prithvi|Earth]].
