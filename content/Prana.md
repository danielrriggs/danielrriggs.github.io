Alias: Qi.

Vital life-force energy that flows throughout us. (This is the difference between something that's living & a rock).

Closely connected to the breath according to the [[Yoga|Yogic]] system.

Qi is the vital life-force energy according to Chinese traditional medicine and martial arts.

Prana is the vital energy required to bring anything into motion. Everything in the entire cosmos is filled with Prana. Every object that has a material form has prana. Prana can be tangible and intangible. It is the very nature of prana to be everywhere around you. It is because of Prana that the wind blows, people talk, corporations rise and fall, ideas spread, businesses bloom, health improves, planets revolve in perfect motion. Creation floats in the unending sea of Prana.

When your Prana moves, your [[Chitta]] moves. Without the movement of prana, the movement of [[Chitta]] would be impossible. Without prana or [[Vasana]], consciousness would fall apart. A living being that has prana and [[Vasana]], experiencing any level of [[Consciousness]], is known as [[Prani]].

For life to exist, for any life form to come into creation, prana and consciousness should come together. Consciousness is what you are aware of. Prana is becoming aware of that. Everything is a combination of prana and chitta. From the [[Mahaprana]], comes into being the singular prana.

Prana is the primary ingredient in all [[Creation]].

###### Moving Prana
Think about your life; your relationships, your financial situation, your health, and see how your chitta is interacting with those aspects of your life. Think about the thoughts, your awareness, and your individual understanding of these situations. The moment your chitta focuses on the negative aspect of your relationship, for example, your prana begins to move in that direction, to manifest exactly the things you focus upon.

You need to use your chitta to move your prana in that direction. However, when you lack prana, or when some aspects of your life lack pranic energy, you will naturally flow towards the negative aspects of life. It will be easier to pull you down during conversations. It would be easier to make your relationships crumble. Or to make you financially dependent. But oppositely, if you have high prana, everything in your life will arrange itself, "naturally / magically", and where actions have to be taken, you will be called forth by your own subconscious, in the right direction to get the work done.
