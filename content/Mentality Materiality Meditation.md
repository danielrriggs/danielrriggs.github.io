Comes from the [[Visuddhimagga]].

Helps us understand the nature of ourselves and the world around us. The core of the practice is to examine what comes from the outside world and what comes from the inside world.

In [[Trauma]], we get triggered by things outside of us. The world around us evokes all kinds of reactions within us. We try very hard to create a world around us to craft an existence where the outside world gives me good things. Good news from the outside world makes me happy, and bad news from the outside world makes me unhappy. This is technically false. The purpose of the mentality materiality exercise is to teach you that this very fundamental idea that certain things in the world will make me happy is technically incorrect.

The practice is simple, but very difficult, because of the conditioning of our [[mind]].

It is to notice the materiality of an object outside and the mentality of what I add to it.

If we look at an object, say, a mug. It has certain material qualities. Weight. Smoothness. Color. Then my mind attaches some reaction to the mug. If it's my favorite, touching or seeing the mug will make me happy. If it's a mug that's leaking, it will make me unhappy.

The essence of the mentality materiality exercise is simply to observe what actually exists outside of me and what exists inside of me.

As you practice this, you'll start to notice a couple of different things - everything that is good or bad does not come from the outside. It comes from the inside. The logical understanding that good and bad comes from within is not sufficient to change your level of happiness.

**The practice**: Take anything kind of thing that exists without you - people, objects, sounds - and notice all of the features of that thing. Then pay attention to what your mind attaches to the object.

Any interaction in life is a combination of the material thing outside and the mental thing inside. The practice is to take apart those two things. Once we separate them, the mentality can start to dissolve.

A lot of our [[Dukkha|suffering]] in life is a confusion of the mentality and the materiality.
*These particular words hurt me. The source of the hurt is in the words themselves.* This is wrong. The hurt exists within you.

Clinical treatments for things like [[Trauma]] or [[Phobia]]s are rooted in the principle of the Mentality Materiality exercise. Phobia treatment uses [[Exposure Therapy]].

What controls us in life are our internal reactions in life. If I find something unpleasant, I can't engage in it. If I find something pleasant, I have to keep engaging in it. The source of suffering in life is running away from the unpleasant stuff and running to pleasant stuff. *Studying is boring, video games are fun*. Boring isn't found in the textbook. Fun isn't in the video game. They exist within you.

Ignore logic and pay attention to your experience.

As you notice your own internal reactions, Y=your avoidance of the negative and attraction to the positive naturally start to dwindle. As these start to dwindle, you will gain more control over how you spend your time.
