Out of 50,000 people who survived a genocidal conflict, only a minority of them got [[Post Traumatic Stress Disorder|PTSD]]. [[Trauma]]s can actually make us stronger. This research formed the foundation of post-traumatic growth.

As living organisms, we have systems to negative experiences get stronger on the other side of it.

Researchers isolated five variables that correlate with post-traumatic growth:
1) Appreciation for life
2) See new possibilities
3) Form supportive relationships
4) Sense of personal strength
5) Undergo spiritual change

As we talked about with [[Somatic Illness]]es, the body is a source of bad signals, so our [[Mind]] tries to numb our body. [[Trauma]] manifestations to numb the body can look like substance abuse or can even include things like working out excessively hard.

1)  **Appreciation for life**
When we numb our body, we're severing ourselves from the capacity to **appreciate life**. We can't selectively numb the bad stuff. We numb the good stuff and the bad stuff. Emotions are a big part of appreciating life. Reconnecting with an appreciation for life starts with **stopping the process of numbing**.

2) **See new possibilities**
When we get [[Trauma|traumatized]], we get stuck in the past. For example, in flashbacks, an event in the past is dominating your experience of the present. In the case of unresolved trauma, all the problems from the past exist in the present. As long as we're stuck in the past, it's difficult to consider the future. As we go through the process of developing a narrative, we will start to put those things in the past. **Once the past stops occupying our present**, we can be in the present and **consider the future**.

3) **Form supportive relationships**
A sense of connectedness with other people essentially comes from empathy. What allows me and you to be connected is that we experience the same thing so that there's a resonance between two people.

If you want someone to feel more attracted to you, create a shared emotional experience. To develop friendships, have shared emotional experiences with common cause.

In [[Trauma]], we have [[Hemispheric Lateralization]] which shuts off our emotions. If our emotions are shut off, we can't form connections with other people. Or, the emotions can be uncontrollable, dysregulated, and overwhelming. It's hard to form a shared emotional connection when our emotional system is all out of whack.

4) **Sense of personal strength**
A strong personal sense of [[Nature of Self|self]] can feel foreign when we are [[Trauma|traumatized]]. This is why we devote so much of the guide to the development of [[Identity (trauma)]]. In Trauma, we form conclusions about ourselves that are strong and negative. We're weak, incapable, below average, etc.

Through identity practices we can dissolve our negative sense of self and a more natrual, positive self will float to the surface. You will be able to reconnect with your internal sense of self that is positive and strong.

5) **Undergo spiritual change**
If you want your trauma to make you a stronger person, that some amount of spiritual change is correlated with that.

What does this mean and how do we invoke it? We don't really know. It's just what the research indicates.
