Alias: Addicted

You want to figure out something in your life that's worth not getting drunk & screwing up, because that's fun.

Prohibition (trying to be 'straight') doesn't work. You need an adventure. Something to do. Something worth waking up for. That's the substitute for the addiction. Actually, the addiction is the substitute for your life adventure.

Why do people pursue rewards that don't produce this resonance? They don't have a value hierarchy. The kids on pleasure island default to hedonistic behavior in the local timeframe because they don't have an identity.
