Alias: Memories

[[Memory]] is the fifth [[Vritti]] of the [[Mind]].

It is of two kinds: **conscious memory** and **subconscious memory**. 

**Conscious memory** involves the recollection of things already experienced, recall of past experiences.

**Subconscious memory** is [[Dream]].

Our mind contacts experience objects through the [[Indriya]]s. The next time our [[Indriya]]s contact a similar experience, memory of the past experience arises if the experience is not allowed to escape from the mind ([[Asampramosha]]). If the experience does escape, then memory fails us.
