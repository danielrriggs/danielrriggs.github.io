A system of Yoga that focuses on becoming [[Enlightenment|enlightened]] through the power of contemplation.

Patanjali recommends reflection and enquiry into the real meaning of [[Aum]] [^sutra-1:27][^sutra-1:28][^sutra-1:29]   and also explains much of [[Samkhya]] philosophy (2:20, 21, etc.) as a means to higher realization. He makes it clear that all mental knowledge is limited.
