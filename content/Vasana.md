Means 'to remain,' 'to dwell,' 'to persist (in memory).'

Behavioral tendency or [[Karma|karmic]] imprint which influences the present behavior of a person. Past impression in the [[Mind]].

Mental imprints. Associated with the notion of subtle [[Desire]].

Patanjali defines _vasana_ as a dynamic chain or concatenation of [[Samskara]]s.

They govern the [[Psyche]] unless overcome by [[Tapas]] and [[Nirodha Parinama]].

Vasanas are stored in the [[Causal Body]]. Vasanas lie dormant until their “input” is required by the intellect in order to make a decision about how to interpret and respond to a given stimulus. Each time we engage in a particular activity or indulge a given vasana, we strengthen it.

In this way, over time vasanas gain a sort of life of their own. This is the cause of our desires, attachments, habits, and, in extreme cases, addictions. When they are indulged regularly, vasanas can grow strong enough to assume control of our mind.
