 Why should one find a Guru? There's a saying that when the student is ready, the teacher appears. It's not a complete saying. I want you to know the full saying. When the student is not just ready, but the student is crying, frustrated, at the end of his wits and ready to do something, but don't know what. Full of energy, but frustration.
