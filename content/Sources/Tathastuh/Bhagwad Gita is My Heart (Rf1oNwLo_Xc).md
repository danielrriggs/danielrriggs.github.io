 My teaching is always based on the Bhagavad Gita because of all the scriptures that we have there are wonderful scriptures, all of them, and so on, but it's only the Bhagavad Gita which actually tells you how to run your daily life. It gives you a very, very practical guidance because the knowledge of the Supreme is of course very important. That is the foundation. There is no doubt about it. But we cannot live our daily life on that. And that's why the Bhagavad Gita is both Brahmavidya and Yogashastra.
