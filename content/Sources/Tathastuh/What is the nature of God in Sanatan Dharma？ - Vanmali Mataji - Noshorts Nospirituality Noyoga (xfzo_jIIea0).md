 Well, the foundation of all spirituality has to be the nature of God. What are you worshipping? If you don't know that, then that is a false premise on which you are worshipping. Then the rest of it will be also false. So, the nature of God in the Sanatana Dharma, as you know, is that of Brahman. The Supreme Force is Brahman, which is faceless, formless, timeless, and totally incomprehensible to the mind. Because the mind can only think in the level of time, space, and causality.
