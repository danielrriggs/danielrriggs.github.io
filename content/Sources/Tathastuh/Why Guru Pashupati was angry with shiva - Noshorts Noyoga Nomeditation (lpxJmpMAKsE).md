 God angry with Shiva because after many days of praying also, he has not sent any Guru. I didn't ask him for strength. I didn't ask him for solutions. I said, you just send me a Guru. Give me knowledge. I will do the rest. That was very annoying to me. I said, I am not like your other beggar disciples, beggar devotees begging you for things. I didn't beg you for anything. I said, just give me knowledge. You put me in this situation. I will deal with it like a warrior. Give me knowledge. I was just 12 years old.
