 Sometimes life will offer you a burning coal when you are least prepared. Worse, when you don't even deserve it. Don't burn yourself with that unexpected offering. Instead, use it to strengthen your position to forge ahead. It is neither a walk in the park nor does it come naturally to us to not burn oneself with the burning coal. But it can be learned and mastered. I say it's hard work because a momentary lapse of mindfulness is enough to make us forget all the wisdom in the world and we end up grabbing the cinder, hurting not only ourselves but those on whom we may hurl it.
