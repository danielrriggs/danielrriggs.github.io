 You have to do things that you may or may not agree with in Aghora. The Guru will make you do things that you find difficult. Wherever you're judgmental, whomever you're judging, the Guru will make you experience that same fate of all the people whom you judge. Everyone you've judged, you'll have to then think, Oh my God, I can't judge them anymore because I've gone through the same thing and I did the same. I reacted the same in their situation. So the Guru will put you through so many situations till you're free of judgment. If you judge people for getting married, Aghora will make you get married. If you judge people for having a job, Aghora will make you work in a job. If you judge your father for something, then you'll have the same situation with your child. So those will be given to you as a gift by a Guru to see how much of your ego can take it and how much of you wants to drop that ego fully.
