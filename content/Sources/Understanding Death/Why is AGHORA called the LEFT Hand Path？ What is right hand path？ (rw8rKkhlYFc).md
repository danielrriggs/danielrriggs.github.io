 Guruji, why is Agura called the left hand path and what is right hand path? Agura is a very advanced path which is the left hand path. It's a slippery path because it depends on complete honesty. If you don't work with full honesty, in the Agura path you'll slip and die. In Agura there is no structure. You can ask me what lessons I'm going to teach, I don't know. Until the lessons come. So you just hang around with the teacher until you get lessons. That's left hand path. Right hand path you'll have a syllabus and a structure. Like Siddha Yoga is the right hand path but Agura is the left hand path. You have to master the right hand path then only you can do left hand path.
