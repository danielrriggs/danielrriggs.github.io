 Why do you think there is so much stigma to talk about mental well-being? Because look, we really are not our bodies, this everybody knows. Because our bodies change constantly. Sometimes they are in health, sometimes they are not well. They start aging, they start changing, you lose your hair and so many things happen. So people don't want to be associated with the body. But where to find them? We have to find them in their mental well-being. If they are not able to be mentally consistent, then who is this person? I don't know this person. Because in the morning they spoke to me nicely and in the afternoon they have such a temper. So I don't want to associate with such a person. When you say you have a mental problem, it means that you are going to show up inconsistently in any relationship. You are not going to show up with stability. That's the problem. Therefore it makes everyone insecure that you are not going to show up with stability. That's why when people have a mental illness problem, they would like to keep it under cover and in denial. Because they don't want to be accused, they don't want to lose the stability in their relationships. They don't want to be rejected because they have been unstable. That's why mental health is a taboo always and it always will be. That's not going to change because mental health changes who you are. In physical health you remain the same person as long as your mind is the same. But in mental health you change who you are. So that's a big scary thing.
