 Guruji, to begin this topic, could you explain what is karma and what is its connection with Sanatan Dharma? Okay, I'm glad you asked this because nowadays we seem to think about karma as if it's a system of punishment and justice. But that is really not what karma is. In Sanatan Dharma, karma simply means action. And it's the way and the quality in which you take these actions that determine what kind of experience that you're going to have. It's more about the attitude of the actions that you take. That's why it's called karma. If it were, it wouldn't be called action. If it were a system of consequence, then it's not karma. Then it would be called consequence. It wouldn't be called action.
