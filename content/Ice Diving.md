Take a bucket. Put ice and water in it. Dunk your face in it. Briefly.

It activates the mammalian diving reflex, activating the [[Parasympathetic Nervous System|PNS]], shuts off the [[Sympathetic Nervous System|SNS]]. We also stop breathing and hold our breath, so, we stop hyperventilating.

When your face is dunked in ice, you stop thinking about whatever you were thinking about.
