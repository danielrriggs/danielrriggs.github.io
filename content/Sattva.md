Alias: Sattvic, Sattwic.

One of the [[Gunas]].

The spiritual quality in human beings. The quality that establishes balance, tranquility, and equanimity in life.

Balance.

Characterized by radiance, purity, luminosity, lightness, harmony, and the production of pleasure. It is the purest aspect of the three [[Gunas]].

Knowledge, peace, light.
