Means "loop". Circular. To go around in circles. It's your tendencies. Turnings of the mind. 

Vritti does not literally mean "modification," "state," or "form," but rather "whirlwind" or "vortex."

The [[Consciousness]] has its circular patterns. The attitudes of [[Chitta]], the modes of mind, are called [[Chitta Vritti]]. There are five.

Mental modifications. When we use the term mental modification, we mean the different patterns or personalities of the mind, the different stages, spheres or dimensions of personality.

###### Five Vritis
These are called [[Chitta Vritti]].
1. Correct knowledge ([[Pramana]])
2. Incorrect knowledge ([[Viparyaya]])
3. Imagination or fantasy ([[Vikalpa]])
4. Sleep ([[Sleep|Nidra]])
5. Memory ([[Smrti]])
