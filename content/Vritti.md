The [[Consciousness]] has its circular patterns. The attitudes of [[Chitta]], the modes of mind, are called [[Chitta Vritti]]. There are five.

Mental modifications. When we use the term mental modification, we mean the different patterns or personalities of the mind, the different stages, spheres or dimensions of personality.
