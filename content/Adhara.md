Means "pillar", or "foundation".

Sometimes the term "adhara" denotes [[Shakti]], which supports [[Shiva]].


Special points in the [[Sukshma Sarira|Energy Body]] of a person. Described in tantric texts as dvadashantas and shodashantas. They are mainly related to [[Raja Yoga]] (the transformation of [[Consciousness]]).

There are 16 [[gross]] adharas, called sthula-adharas.
###### The 16 sthula-adharas:
Padangushta-adhara — the toes.
Muladhara — the perineum, the [[Muladhara Chakra|Muladhara]] chakra.
Gudadhara — the anus.
Medhradhara — the base of genital, [[Swadhisthana Chakra|Svadhishthana]] chakra.
Uddiyana-adhara — the kanda, lower abdomen.
Nabhi-adhara — the navel area, [[Manipura Chakra|Manipura]] chakra.
Hridaya-adhara — the area of the heart, [[Anahata Chakra|Anahata]] chakra.
Kantha-adhara — the area of ​​the neck, [[Vishuddha Chakra|Vishuddha]] chakra.
Ghantika-adhara — uvula.
Talu-adhara — soft palate, talu-chakra.
Jihva-adhara — tongue.
Bhrumadhya-adhara — the are between eyebrows, [[Ajna Chakra|Ajna]] chakra.
Nasa-adhara — the tip of the nose.
Kapata-adhara — the base of the nose.
Lalata-adhara — the center of the forehead.
Brahmarandhra-adhara — brahmarandhra, [[Akasha]] chakra.
