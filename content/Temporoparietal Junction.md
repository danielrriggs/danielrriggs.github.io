The [[Temporoparietal Junction]] joins the [[Temporal lobe]] and the [[Parietal Lobe]].

Some of these [[Dissociative Disorders|Dissociative]] experiences or [[Dissociative Disorders|Depersonalization]] experiences are triggered by activity in the temporoparietal lobe.

The temporoparietal junction integrates information and gives us an experience of "us". Internal experiences like thoughts, emotions, feelings are being experienced by "us". There's a sense of "you-ness" that's always there, independent of what you experience. This sense of "me-ness" seems to live in the temporoparietal junction.

If we electrically stimulate the temporoparietal junction, we can trigger an out-of-body experience - an experience where people are floating above their body, and they can look down on themselves, and they don't feel anything that is happening to them. It feels like your [[Consciousness]] leaves your body and you can look down upon yourself.

Some esoteric traditions talk about things like [[Astral Travel]] and things like that.
