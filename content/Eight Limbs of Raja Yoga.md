Described in the [[Yoga Sutra]]s of Patanjali.

In [[Raja Yoga]]/[[Raja Yoga|Ashtanga Yoga]], there are 8 prerequisites to attain a state of [[Meditation]]:

1) [[Yama]]
2) [[Niyama]]
3) [[Asana]]
4) [[Pranayama]]
5) [[Pratyahara]]
The first five are exoteric, or [[Bahiranga]]. They prepare the body-mind for  the last 3 stages:
7) [[Dharana]]
8) [[Dhyana]]
9) [[Samadhi]]
The last 3 expand consciousness.

The [[Ida]] is balanced with the [[Pingala]] so that the [[Sushumna]] begins to function in [[Samadhi]].

The eight stages steady the five [[Kosha]]s of man. See [[Panchakosha Theory]].

###### The eight steps are for advanced aspirants
 They're advanced techniques for those people who have exhausted most of their mental problems and conflicts. They aren't really for the average person. In the beginning of the [[Yoga Sutra]]s it says "Now, therefore, complete instructions regarding yoga." The words 'now therefore' refer to extensive preparations.

From a text prior to the [[Yoga Sutra]]s, the [[Hatha Yoga Pradipika]] it says:

> Prostrations to Shiva who taught [[Hatha]] Yoga as a ladder to climb to the peak of [[Raja Yoga]]. Because of confusion and misconceptions, most people are unable to practice [[Raja Yoga]]; in compassion, Swatmarama (the author) describes [[Hatha]] Yoga as a torch to remove ignorance. (1:1-3)
> 
> [[Hatha]] Yoga in the form of [[Asana]]s, [[Pranayama]] and other practices should be mastered until one is ready for [[Raja Yoga]]. (1:67)
> 

Without this preparation, one makes no headway with [[Patanjali Yoga]]. One reason the eight steps are advanced is that they ask people to restrain their thoughts; for most people, who have disturbed minds, this would do more harm than good. It is not advisable to try to suppress the compelling thoughts and desires that still exist in the mind of the average person.

The basic rules of [[Yama]] and [[Niyama]] are very difficult for the average person. These rules are really for those people who have exhausted most of their [[Samskara]]s and [[Karma]]s. For example, if most people try to practice [[Brahmacharya]], they will become neurotics.

First, one should take steps to purify the mind through [[Hatha]] Yoga, [[Karma]] Yoga, [[Bhakti Yoga]] and general [[Meditation|Meditative]] techniques that involve awareness more than concentration.
