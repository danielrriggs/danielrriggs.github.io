Grounding techniques cause a person to focus on the present, ground a person in a particular physical sensation. Anything with a sensory input which draws your attention. 

Makes thoughts flee your [[Mind]] because your attention focuses on the [[Indriya]].

- Hold an ice cube
- Walk through the grass with no shoes
- Hold a warm cup of tea
- Sauna
- Music
- Smells
- Ice Diving - dunk your face in ice water
