We avoid what we fear.

We want to expose ourselves to the thing that triggers us in a safe environment until we **acclimatize** to it. Any time the body or the brain receives a stimulus over and over and over again, if we function the way we're supposed to, we actually drown out that stimulus.

*For example, The moment you put on a shirt, you can feel the shirt. After awhile, you numb yourself to the feeling of the shirt.*
