Alias: Intelligence.

The [[Yoga]] word for intelligence is [[Vijnana]] - The ability to acquire and **apply** knowledge and skills. 

Anybody who can acquire knowledge is not intelligent.
Anybody who can only apply the knowledge that they have without acquiring new knowledge is not intelligent.
If you're applying your knowledge and you're not growing, you're not learning new things, you're not acquiring new knowledge, then also you're not intelligent.

[[Buddhi]] is intellect, not intelligence.

V means opposite. As in [[Viparita]] or [[Virodha]].
Marriage is [[Vivaha]] - it is the V (opposite) of [[Vaha]] flow. Anything that stops your flow is called marriage.

So, according to [[Yoga]], intelligence is what challenges knowledge. To challenge the knowledge that you already have.

The intelligence [[Kosha]] of a [[Human Being]] is the [[Vijnanamaya Kosha]].

###### The head of the [[Vijnanamaya Kosha]] is [[Sraddha|Shraddha]] (faith).
Faith is the first part of intelligence.

In order to have faith, confidence, and belief, you must have understanding. This **understanding** is called [[Samskara]]. The basis of understanding is stored in you. If you don't have understanding (of the nature of something) then you can't have [[Sraddha|Faith]] (which kills fear).

The **skill** of **understanding** is the skill to change [[Samskara]]s.

Fearlessness is one of the biggest signs of an intelligent person. To be fearless is to increase your understanding so that the fear goes away. Never fight your fear.  If you fight your fear, it will stay in your subconscious and in the moment where you really need focus & stable it will re-emerge & you will be shaken.

###### The foot of the [[Vijnanamaya Kosha]] is rhythm
Rhythm is the second part of intelligence.

Rhythm means doing right according to your conscience.

To have rhythm, you need the **skill** of rejecting others (in a compassionate way). In order to listen to your conscience, you need to be able to **say no to people**.

The conscience is a compass that points toward meaning in life. If you follow your rhythm with less effort you will get more results.

###### Satya
Satya is the third part of intelligence.

Saying what is without sugarcoating (compassionately) - no deceit; no denial.

It's the **skill** of handling rejection without getting hurt (your confidence level).

###### Yoga
[[Yoga]] is the fourth part of intelligence - [[Yoga]] means to feel worthy - that you have ripened.

How do you feel worthy? Start measuring your impact. Accurately measuring your impact is a **skill**. The skill of knowing. Then you'll know where you stand. Always know where you stand. A lot of people don't know where they stand in the world. Due to fear of rejection.  If you get the **skill** of handling rejection without being hurt, then you can measure your impact.

###### Maha
[[Maha]] is the fifth part of intelligence.

Maha means to [[Prioritize]]. Vigilance. The **skill** is to prioritize your happiness. To take full responsibility for your life and the **skill** of getting rid of all obstacles. Whatever the obstacles to your happiness are, you should have the skill to clear them. You need good relationships, good health, good wealth.

###### Summary
- [[Sraddha|Shraddha]] (faith) - The skill of understanding (changing [[Samskara]]s).
- Rhythm - The skill of rejecting others in a compassionate way.
- [[Satya]] - The skill of handling rejection without getting hurt
- [[Yoga]] - The skill of accurately measuring your impact to know where you stand
- [[Maha]] - The skill of vigilance

There is only one good decision. What you're meant to do at this point of time. What you're designed to do at this point of time. That's the good decision. When you have [[Sraddha|Shraddha]], you will start developing rhythm automatically.
