There are 3 Nadis:
- [[Ida]] - left
- [[Pingala]] - right
- [[Sushumna]] - center

Nadi doesn't mean nerve. Nadis are channels of [[Prana]]. In the [[Pranamaya Kosha|Pranamayakosha]] body, there are 72,000 Nadis - there are 72,000 ways [[Prana]] moves.

The [[Ida]] and [[Pingala]] represent the basic duality in the existence. We traditionally personify them as [[Shiva]] and [[Shakti]]. These are qualities in nature. Some qualities in nature have been identified as [[Shiva|Masculine]]; others, [[Shakti|Feminine]].

Most people live and die in [[Ida]] and [[Pingala]]; [[Sushumna]], the central space, remains dormant.
