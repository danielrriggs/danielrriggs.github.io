The gateway of the brain. The part of our brain that all of the other parts of our body and brain connect to. Like the central station of traffic from all of the different parts of our brain and our body.

For example, all of our sensation of physical touch, temperature, our eyesight, the sounds that we hear - all first meet in the thalamus and then connect to different parts of our brain.

Our thalamus also gets inputs from our emotional centers, our cognitive centers. It's like the information hub of our brain.
