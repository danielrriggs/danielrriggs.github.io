Alias: fear of death.

Fear of death. The urge to not die.

Which is sustained by mistaking the self for the voice in the head that declares itself "me" [[Asmita]]. All of these problems are driven by ignorance about our true nature [[Avidya]].

Don't waste willpower on things you don't want to do. Save it for overcoming fear in life and death situations. You can't self generate feelings.

It's believed we have 5 sources of [[Dukkha|suffering]] ([[Klesha]]s). One of these is the fear of death. In yogic literature, they believe all our fears come out of this single fear of death. The majority of our fears stem from a limited amount of time.
	*I'm not using my time wisely; I'm wasting too much time on this relationship; I'm getting older; life is passing me by.*

The two guarantees in life are death and [[Dukkha|suffering]] (according to [[Buddha]]).
