# **Building a Fulfilling Career**

*January 27, 2025*

## **Outline:**

- Reflect on how internalized desires and societal conditioning shape your career goals.  
- Assess the impact of external factors like burnout, lack of growth opportunities, and workload on your career satisfaction.  
- Examine internal challenges such as self-comparison, unrealistic expectations, and avoiding responsibility for your own growth.  
- Identify your natural strengths by exploring what genuinely interests you and strategize how to handle less enjoyable aspects of your job.  
- Evaluate what you bring to the table, including your skills, mindset, and emotional tendencies, to better understand your unique value.  
- Take time to reflect on your feelings about a job or your overall career before making significant decisions to gain clarity and direction.

## **Weekly Quests:**

### Light Quest – Aligning Work with Core Values

*This quest helps you gain clarity on your core values and how they align with your daily actions. By identifying your top 3 values, you can make more intentional decisions about how you spend your time and energy, ensuring that your job and activities resonate with your deeper needs. This process can improve job satisfaction, increase motivation, and create a sense of fulfillment by aligning your actions with what truly matters to you.*

**Activity**– fill out the Core Value Table below with the following steps.

1. **Search for a "list of values"** to explore a variety of values.  
2. **Choose 10 values** from the list that resonate with you and feel important.  
3. **Narrow down** those 10 values **to your top 3**—these are your core values.  
4. **Reflect** on your daily tasks and write down specific ways you can incorporate these core values more into your life and work.

Understanding these factors will enable you to make more conscious decisions about your career, whether that means making adjustments in your current role or considering a shift*

**Activity: reflect on your current job by listing your role, daily tasks, and dividing them into what you like and dislike.** Consider whether the "likes" align with the core of your job. *This quest helps you reflect on how past experiences shape your emotions and actions in current roles or projects. By identifying what you bring to the table—both in terms of strengths and challenges—you can gain valuable insights into your behavior patterns and improve future experiences, whether that’s better communication, managing expectations, or aligning your role with your values.*

**Activity– reflect on your past work experiences by listing the last three jobs or group projects.** For each one, explore your feelings before starting, how they changed over time, and what you liked or disliked about the experience. Finally, analyze your contributions to both the positive and negative aspects of those roles, considering how past emotions or assumptions influenced your behavior and interactions and thus slowly shaped your career trajectory.
