## **Weekly Quest**

This quest continues the previous lecture, Building Meaning Friendship Pt 1, and is a bit lighter than the Pt.1 weekly quest. This quest is designed to dust off those social skills and engage in genuine, reciprocal connections. Continue to practice these skills in your sangha\! 

**Objective:** To engage in meaningful, face-to-face interactions that build social confidence and enhance non-verbal communication skills.

**Exercise:** Emotional Exploration 

Reflect on your emotional experiences in interacting with others. Explore the origins and how they’ve shaped your views on relationships. You can use the table below or create your own journal entry. 

**Journal Prompt:** 

* What are common emotional experiences you have when interacting with others? (e.g., happiness, discomfort, excitement, apprehension)  
* For each emotion listed, do a samskaric exploration of its origin. Can you trace these feelings back to specific events or experiences in your life?  
* Based on your emotional exploration, what conclusions have you formed about people and relationships? Are these conclusions helping or hindering your ability to form meaningful connections?  
* Write a brief narrative about a key lesson you've learned regarding social interactions and relationships. How did you come to learn this lesson, and how has it influenced your approach to forming connections?
