Over the next week, set aside time on three different days to reflect on and answer the following questions about your experiences and perspectives as a man. Write down your thoughts either on paper or in a notes app.

You can do it all in one sitting if you want, or over 2-3 days. It’s best to be in a calm state of mind before attempting this. You can meditate or take a walk beforehand to get into the right state of mind.

The point of this exercise is so that you are one step closer to live a life that is aligned with what you want. Men are often stuck between living up to other's expectations, and as you learn more about yourself on a deeper level, you gain a sense of agency and become more aware of the choices you have.

* What are the advantages or privileges you have experienced as a man? If you find this challenging, use this as an opportunity for reflection. The more difficult it is to articulate, the more it can enhance your cognitive flexibility.  
* List the expectations you face as a man. What makes these expectations challenging to meet?  
* Reflect on which of these expectations are self-imposed and which have been conditioned by society. BONUS: Ask your parents what they expect of you. That will give you a better idea of what you have been conditioned towards.  
* Choose three specific expectations you have identified. Think about how you can either embrace or let go of these expectations.

Once you have identified the expectations that have been placed on you, you have a choice. You can embrace some of them and do your best to knock it out of the park. You can also move towards shedding the ones you don’t want to live up to.

You don't need to share these reflections with anyone, but they can be a valuable tool for personal growth. If you're involved in any form of personal coaching, such as HG Personal or Group Coaching, consider discussing your insights with your coach or group. After writing your reflections, revisit them after a day to see if your perspectives have shifted or if new thoughts have emerged
