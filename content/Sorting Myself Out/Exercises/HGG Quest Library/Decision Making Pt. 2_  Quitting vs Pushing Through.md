# **Decision Making Pt. 2:**  **Quitting vs Pushing Through**

*April 16, 2025*

# Outline

- **There’s no “right” choice:** No one can see the future, so focus on making conscious, value-based decisions instead of chasing the perfect outcome.  
- **Regret comes from ignoring the cost:** We often regret choices not because of the outcome, but because we weren’t aware of what we were giving up. Knowing your trade-offs reduces future regret.  
- **Attachments cloud clarity:** Emotional ties to relationships, identity, or past investments (like sunk costs) can distort our ability to make clear decisions.  
- **Emotions need to be regulated, not avoided:** Understand your feelings by asking what they are, where they come from, and what they’re urging you to do — then decide with clarity, not impulse.  
- **True clarity comes from within:** Quieting your mind helps you hear your inner voice. When logic and emotion are balanced, the heart often knows when to push forward or when to let go.

## Medium Quest–  Sorting Through the Emotion & Attachment Fog

*Identify how attachments and emotions may be clouding your decision-making.* 

**Activity:** Choose a decision you’re struggling with. Use the table below to untangle emotional noise from inner clarity.

1. **List the Decision(s):**  
    Choose 1–2 decisions you’re currently struggling with.  
2. **Vomit Journal:**  
    Let all your thoughts spill onto the page. Don’t filter—capture doubts, hopes, fears, reasons, and what-ifs.  
3. **Analyze the Journal:**  
- What emotions are present?  
- What attachments (ego, identity, fear, sunk cost, approval, etc.) might be influencing this?  
4. **Reframe Without Emotion or Attachment:**  
    Ask: *“If I wasn’t feeling these emotions or attached to this outcome, what would I choose?”.* This brings you closer to your heart's truth—calm, wise, and uncluttered by fear.

## Advanced Quest –  Kaya Shtiram: Cultivating Stillness and Mental Discipline

For the next 10 days, practice Kaya Shtiram in 3 stages:

**Stage 1 (Days 1-3):**

* Practice 5 minutes daily.  
* Focus on staying completely still, observing any urges to move without acting on them.

**Stage 2 (Days 4-6):**

* Increase to 10 minutes daily.  
* Continue observing sensations and practicing non-reaction.

**Stage 3 (Day 7 onwards):**

* Practice 15 minutes daily and whenever you feel lustful.  
* Use this practice when experiencing strong lustful urges to regain control.

**Kaya Shtiram– How to Practice :**

1. Find a quiet, comfortable space where you won’t be disturbed.  
2. Sit or lie down in a stable, relaxed position.  
3. Close your eyes and keep your body completely still. Resist urges to move, adjust, laugh, or scratch an itch.  
4. Observe any sensations or impulses that arise without reacting to them.  
5. Breathe slowly and deeply, optionally using your breath to anchor your focus and calm your mind.
