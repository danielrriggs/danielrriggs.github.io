## **Weekly Quest**

This quest continues the previous lecture, Deep Dive into Detachment Pt 1, and is a bit lighter than the Pt.1 weekly quest. Continue with the Pt. 1 quest or try something new. 

**Objective:** To practice detachment in everyday situations by improving self-awareness and detachment.

#### **Lite Quests:** 

**Mindfulness Meditation**

Continue your meditation practive. Spend 10 minutes, aim for 3xs this week, practicing mindfulness, focusing solely on your breathing and observing thoughts as they come and go without attachment or judgment.

####  **Medium Quest:** 

**Socratic Questioning for Self-Reflection**

Question your core beliefs by asking deep, reflective questions about the evidence supporting these beliefs, possible alternatives, and the realistic outcomes of thinking this way. This helps in detaching from rigid perceptions driven by the ego.

1. List a belief you hold strongly  
2. Start by asking yourself the provided questions below to explore this belief  
     
   **Questions**  
* What evidence do I have for this belief?   
* Are there alternatives to consider?   
* How does my perspective influence my understanding of this evidence? 

**Building your Meditation Mindfulness**

Pick a meditation practice (ie. nadi shodhana, trataka, neti neti, vipassana, etc.) and commit to a daily practice of 15 minutes, or longer if you have been meditating for awhile. Try to be fully into the meditation. Notice what arises just notice it. Later you can write down any insights or observations in a journal. 

#### **Advanced Quest: ** 

Observe a debate or discussion without taking sides. Reflect on how it feels to remain detached. 

**Steps:** 

* Try first with a topic you are slightly interested in.    
* Progress to topics and discussions that you are deeply invested in, including personal relationships

**Conversation: Discussing Household Responsbilities**

| Key Points from Side A | Key Points from Side B | Personal Reflection on Detachment |
| ----- | ----- | ----- |
| *Feels overwhelmed with current distribution of tasks and requests more help Wants to discuss more equitable sharing of tasks.* | *Believes the current system works well and sees no need for change. Feels that efforts are being overlooked and underappreciated.*  | *Remaining detached in a personal discussion about household chores was difficult, especially since it affects my daily life. However, by not taking sides, even internally, I could listen more genuinely to the concerns of my partner. It was a practice in patience and understanding, and it opened up a pathway to discuss changes without conflict.* |

**Daily Life Detachment Log**

For one week, practice observing without casting judgment to cultivate an understanding and non-attachment.

**Daily Log:**

* Describe instances where you would typically make a judgment.  
* Practice perceiving the situation without adding personal interpretation.  
* Note down insights or shifts in perception.

**Log Template:**

| Date | Action Taken | Immediate Judgment | Non-Judgmental Perception | Insights Gained |
| ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- |
| *4/22* | *Ally made a tactical error* | *What the F? They’re not skilled* | *They took a risk and it didn’t pay off* | *Risk-taking is part of growth* |
| *4/23* | *Coworker was late to the meeting* | *Assumed laziness or disorganization* | *They arrived without making excuses and got straight to work.* | *Perhaps they had a valid reason for being late; promptness isn't the only indicator of professionalism.* |

**Reflection:**

* What patterns do you notice in your immediate judgments?  
* How has this exercise shifted your perception throughout the week?
