# **Understanding Shame**

*September 4, 2024*

## **Outline:**

* Understand how shame develops across different developmental stages such as toddler years, puberty, and adulthood.  
* Recognize how shame complexes manifest in adulthood as either positive or negative ahamkaras.  
* Distinguish between healthy and unhealthy shame: healthy shame is an emotion that guides us to corrective action, while unhealthy shame forms an identity that we carry around  
* Heal shame by identifying and accepting it; the problem with shame arises when we hide or reject parts of ourselves.  
* Explore methods for healing shame, such as self-reflection, being seen by others, recognizing shame patterns, and adopting meditative practices that align with your goals.

## Medium Quests – Spiritual Practices

### Connect with Our Whole Self through Metta Meditation

*Meditation is a process of reconnecting with our true, whole self. Since shame often involves rejecting parts of ourselves, meditative practices can help us foster self-acceptance. Metta Meditation (loving-kindness meditation), in particular, focuses on cultivating compassion, kindness, and goodwill towards yourself and others.*

**Activity:** Practice Nadi Shodhana for 5-10 minutes, followed by a 10 \- 15 min Metta meditation, bringing awareness to the change in your mind and emotions. 

**Instructions for Metta Meditation:**

1. **Sit Comfortably:** Keep your spine straight and close your eyes.  
2. **Choose Your Metta Phrases:** Common phrases include:  
   * “May you be happy.”  
   * “May you be free.”  
   * “May you be at peace.”  
3. **Set an Intention:** Focus on fostering loving-kindness, beginning with someone you appreciate, then extending it to yourself, and finally to those you may have negative feelings with  
4. **Begin with Someone You Appreciate:** Picture this person in your mind and silently repeat the metta phrases three times.  
5. **Move to Yourself:** Visualize yourself in the room, and repeat the same phrases three times to yourself.  
6. **Extend to Someone You Have Negative Feelings Toward:** Compassionately direct the phrases of kindness toward the person, repeating the phrases sincerely three times.

## Advanced Quests – Introspective Practices on Shame

1. ### Notice the Pattern of Shame 

*A pattern of shame often begins with a negative experience. This experience leads to identity formation, which is reinforced by additional negative experiences and thoughts, perpetuating the cycle.*  
Notic  
**Activity:** Identify 3 significant situations where you suspect this pattern of shame is present.

| Situation / Event | Internal Thoughts | (Negative) Emotions  | (Discouraged) Actions | Thoughts and Emotions Further Influenced by Actions |
| ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- |
| *There’s a job opening under my boss* | *My boss always dismisses me. He/She doesn't like me. I don’t get a chance.* | *Feels dismissed, unimportant, hopeless.*  | *Don’t apply for the job. Be cold to the boss.* | *My boss treats me exactly as I thought. Others always get opportunities while I don’t* |
| *Friend cancels plans last minute* | *I’m not important enough to them* | *Hurt, neglected, unworthy* | *Withdraw from reaching out.* | *Feel more isolated; anticipate further neglect from others.* |
| *Miscommunication with a colleague* | *They don’t value my contributions.* | *Frustration, undervalued* | *Avoid future interactions.* | *Miscommunications continue; feel more alienated and undervalued.* |

2. ### Recognize the Shame Manifestation

3. ### Understand Why Shame is the "Raid Boss" of Emotions

*Shame is often a deeply layered emotion, amplifying other feelings like guilt, anger, or inadequacy. By recognizing these emotional layers, we can better understand how shame influences our reactions.*

**Activity:** Identify emotions that tend to get layered with shame. List 3 emotions you experience often and explore if they are strengthened by shame by adding "should" to your thoughts.

| Situation / Event | Internal Feelings | Thoughts | Is Shame Involved? |
| ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- |
