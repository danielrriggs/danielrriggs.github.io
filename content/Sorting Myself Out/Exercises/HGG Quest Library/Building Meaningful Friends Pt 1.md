## **Weekly Quest**

This quest encourages you to identify connections that can support your journey toward self-improvement and meaningful relationships. 

**Objective:** Identify and interact with at least one new community or group that aligns with your interest. 

**Exercise:** Who do you want to be? 

Let’s think through how you can cultivate relationships to move in the direction of who you want to be. We want you to be able to identify the people who hold you back and those who help you move forward. Then, we want you to contemplate how can you find more people to help you move forward? 

1. If you don’t have this already, create a detailed vision of who you want to be, focusing on personal qualities, values, and goals. You can do this creatively with a vision board or write it out.   
2. Now, list the people you interact with most frequently.  
3. For each person, note whether they hold you back or help you move forward relative to your identity vision.   
4. See if you can notice patterns or traits in people that either support or hinder your progress.   
5. Based on your audit, how can you find more people with the traits that help you move toward who you want to be?

**Reflection:** After this activity, take a moment to think about what you can do with the information you have now uncovered. 

1. Is there a relationship you want to strengthen? If so, what is an activity you could do to enhance this relationship? 

2. Express gratitude to those who have a positive impact on your life. You can journal about it, you can write them a thank you note/message, or you can do a small gesture of appreciation. How does expressing your gratitude affect your mood? 

**Exercise:** Digital Detox Day\!

1. Spend half a day or one whole day without any digital communication. Use this time to connect with others in person. 

2. Journal your observations on social interactions without the digital crutch.

**Journal Prompt:** 

* How did your social interactions differ without a digital device?   
* Did you notice any changes to your ability to communicate or connect with others? 

## Side Quest

These are shorter quests to help supplement your reflections by giving you additional ways to test your understanding and perception. As the quests above, these are optional but fun to do with others\! 

**Exercise:** Understanding Body Language

Watch a silent film or scenes from a silent film with the volume off and interpret the story and emotions based solely on the actors' body language.

**Silent Film Clip:** [Charlie Chaplin - Modern Times - Roller Skating Scene](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kPcEFHA3X0c) (3 min clip)

* What emotion do the actors have when entering the store? How do you know?   
* What are the woman’s feelings once she sees the guy roller skating?   
* What is the guy's emotion towards the end of the clip? How do you know? 

Try to be patient with yourself and others during this process. Consistency and small efforts help in cultivating meaningful connections. The quests are not just for completing tasks but also for your own journey of discovering who YOU are and building a supportive community.
