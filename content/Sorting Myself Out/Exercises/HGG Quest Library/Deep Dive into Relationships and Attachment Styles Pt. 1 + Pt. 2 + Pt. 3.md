## **Weekly Quests**

This quest explores how your past and present relationships shape your attachment style and provide tools for healing and growth. 

**1\. Noticing Your Attachment Style**

**Activity**  
Based on your understanding of avoidant, anxious, and secure attachment styles, look at the chart below ascertain your partners’ attachment style. Keep in mind that a person could express avoidant, anxious, and secure behavior at different times. Try to notice what behaviors they express the most.

| Avoidant Behavior | Anxious Attachment | Secure Attachment |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Mixed signals | Desired closeness | Reliable and consistent |
| Values independence | Struggles with conflict | Can make relationship decisions together |
| Finds problems in others | Expresses insecurities and is worried about them | Can communicate about the relationship |
| Idealized the past | Tries to keep you close through whatever means | Not afraid of commitment |
| Paranoid or mistrustful | Tendency to act out | Enjoys the relationship |
| Uncompromising perspectives in relationships | Has difficulty with theory of mind | Comfortable with blending of lives |
| Withdraws after fights | Tends to take things personally |  |
| Has difficulty articulating emotions and experiences | Spends a lot of time thinking of the relationships |  |

**Reflection**

* What is your partner’s attachment style?  
  * How does it affect your relationship?  
* What is your attachment style?  
  * How does it affect your relationship?  
* Are there situations where the interaction of your partner and your attachment style lead to problems? What are the signs?  
* If you are not in a relationship, what attachment style did your previous partners have?  
* What attachment style do you find yourself attracted to?
