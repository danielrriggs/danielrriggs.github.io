### Medium Quest – Catching “Non-Self-Loving” Moments

**Activity: Over the next 3 days, take note of any moments when you feel stuck, negative, or critical toward yourself.** Often, these “non-self-loving” moments happen automatically, so it may take practice to catch them as they occur\!

**Steps:**

1. **Capture**: Capture any self-critical or negative thoughts, especially those starting with “I.” Write down as many thoughts as possible before moving to the next step.  
2. **Reframe**: Restate each thought in a compassionate way. A simple starter can be adding a “not” in your statements to shift perspectives.  
3. **Reflect**: Take an observer’s perspective, noticing how you originally spoke to yourself. Consider possible triggers or deeper patterns behind the thoughts.

**Activity: Over the next 5-7 days, dedicate at least 20 minutes a day to any body-oriented Anahata Chakra meditation and record your meditation time in the below table.** If you don’t have an established routine, follow below steps that focus on connecting with the energy of your heart center and expanding feelings of love and compassion.

**Steps**:

1. **Settle and Focus**  
   Begin by sitting comfortably. Close your eyes and take a few deep, slow breaths. As you relax, direct your focus to your spine, grounding yourself in the present.

2. **Feel the Flow of Energy**  
   Visualize a gentle, flowing energy starting at the base of your spine. Let it slowly rise, filling each part of your body. As the energy reaches your heart center (Anahata chakra), imagine it "filling" your chest with warmth and light. Sense this flow as a powerful yet calm waterfall, steady and endless, moving outward from your heart.

3. **Expand with Divine Love**  
   As the warmth in your chest grows, picture it expanding beyond you. See yourself as an instrument of divine love, allowing this compassionate energy to flow freely from your heart, touching everything around you.

4. **Sensory Awareness**  
   Bring your attention to the sensations in your chest. Notice any contrast between the front of your sternum and your back. Observe feelings of warmth, hollowness, or fullness—where the energy flows freely or where there may be emptiness inviting warmth and healing energy.

5. **Rest in Presence**  
   Allow the energy to flow naturally. Observe it without force or expectation. If your mind wanders, gently bring it back to the heart, feeling the warmth and openness there.

6. **Closing**  
   When you’re ready, slowly bring your awareness back to your breath. Take a few gentle, grounding breaths, then gradually open your eyes.

**Bonus:** Throughout your day, pay attention to any feelings of warmth, hollowness, or expansion similar to those you experienced in meditation. Noticing these sensations will help bring into your daily life the heart-centered awareness and compassionate energy you developed in meditation.
