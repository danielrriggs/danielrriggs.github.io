# **Deep Dive into Learning Pt. 1 \+ Pt. 2**

*Jun 19, 2024*

## **Outline:**

* Understanding learning and the absorption of information  
  * Dependent on hippocampal circuits, focus, salience, memory capacity, and attention during learning  
* Hippocampus and how output solidifies learning  
  * Capacity to understand, manipulate information, and make predictions  
* Effective learning strategies:   
  * Engage, explore, and make connections  
  * Consider practical applications of learning   
  * Reward and maintain curiosity  
* Stress and how it can enhance focus and salience needs to be balanced.   
  * Balance of PNS and SNS activity is essential for effective learning  
* Integrating and sustaining curiosity through its phases:   
  * Situation  
  * Maintenance  
  * internal storage  
  * Recognition of others’ opinions  
* Yogic perspective on learning:   
  * The focusing of the mind leads to deep learning  
  * Differentiates between transmissible information (vidya) and understanding (jnana)  
  * Experience and unlearning are crucial for deep understanding.   
* Practical tips for learning:   
  * Study in the morning, take naps, and ensure good sleep  
  * Focus on the “how, when, where” of studying  
  * Reframe information as a reward  
  * Brain has a limited capacity for processing and solidifying information  
* Ego and learning:  
  * Learning from mistakes is a powerful stimulator  
  * Tolerance of negative affect and ego management are critical  
  * Divest ego-based identity from the learning endeavor

## **Weekly Quest**

This quest explores how to improve your learning skills with reflective practices, meditation, and noticing your own innate triggers. 

**Objective:**  To apply effective learning strategies and integrate meditation practices to improve focus, retention, and overall learning experience.

**Lite Quests:**

**Curiosity Trigger:**  
Identify a topic of interest and research it. Write down what initially sparked your curiosity and how you can maintain interest.

| Topic | Initial Spark | Maintain Interest |
| ----- | ----- | ----- |
| *Psychology* | *Watching a TED talk on human bevior* | *Read related books and talk with others in discord* |

**Information as Reward:**  
Choose a topic you are passionate about and research it deeply. Treat the acquisition of information as a reward and note how it affects your motivation. Journal about the experience: 

* Why did you choose this topic?   
* How did you feel about the topic before and then after your research?   
* How did you treat this as a reward, and did that work? Why or why not? What can you change?   
* Were there any changes in your motivation as you learned more about the topic?   
* Are there any immediate actions you can take based on what you learned?   
* What insights have you gained about your learning habits? 

**Pranayama (Breathing Exercises):**  
Practice alternate nostril breathing or Bhramari (bee breath) for 5 minutes a day. Preferably, do this in the morning or right before studying, but it can be done at any time. If you meditate, do this before your meditation. 

**Medium Quest:**

**Morning Study Routine:**  
Implement a morning study routine for a week. Create a simple daily ritual (puja) that signifies the beginning and end of a study session, such as lighting a candle or incense. Keep a log of your study activities and reflect on their effectiveness. What can you change for the next session to make it more enjoyable? 

| Date | Ritual | What you learned | How will you apply your learning? | What can you change?  |
| ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- |
| *6/19* | *Light a candle* | *New AI prompting for ChatGPT* | *I will try to create a website using ChatGPT* | *Try the pomodoro technique for better focus* |

**Ego-Free Learning Challenge:**  
Engage in a challenging activity (e.g., a new game or skill) where you expect to make mistakes. Reflect on how managing your ego and focusing on learning impacts your progress.

| Activity | Expected Mistakes | Mistakes Made | Impact on learning |
| ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- |
| *Learning to play guitar* | *Incorrect finger placement* | *Frequently hit wrong notes* | *Improved understanding of musical theory and finger dexterity* |

**Mistake Analysis:**  
Document a recent mistake in learning or work. Analyze what went wrong, what you learned from it, and how you can improve.

* **Mistake:** *I misinterpreted a key concept in my biology class.*  
* **Analysis**: *I didn’t fully read the material and jumped to conclusions.*  
* **Improvements:** *Take more time to read and understand the material thoroughly before forming conclusions.* 

**Meditation (Dhyana and Visualization (Bhavana):**  
Set aside 10-20 minutes daily for meditation to focus the mind and reduce stress. At the end of your meditation, spend a few minutes visualizing the successful application of what you have learned. 

**Advanced Quest:**

1. **Stress Management Plan:** First, develop a personal plan to manage stress during learning sessions. Include techniques such as mindfulness, exercise, and time management.  
2. **Integrating and Sustaining Curiosity:** Second, choose a topic of curiosity and create a study routine. You can use the template in the Medium Quest section. Then, implement the phases of turning curiosity around your topic into sustained interest: situational, maintenance, internal storage, and recognition of others’ opinions. This involves a longer-term commitment to maintaining and building on curiosity.  
3. **Detachment (Vairagya):** Cultivate a sense of detachment from the outcome of learning, focusing on the process rather than results.   
4. **Reflection:** After each session, reflect on how these practices are impacting your learning abilities and make adjustments as needed.
