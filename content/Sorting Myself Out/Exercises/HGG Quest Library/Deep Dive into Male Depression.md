### **Weekly Quest**

**DISCLAIMER**: MANAGEMENT OF CLINICAL DEPRESSION REQUIRES A PROFESSIONAL INTERVENTION. THIS EXERCISE IS NOT MEANT TO BE A SUBSTITUTE FOR MENTAL HEALTH TREATMENT FROM A LICENSED MENTAL HEALTH PROFESSIONAL.

The goal of these exercises is to boost your awareness of the different coping mechanisms that tend to manifest more in men.

Pick one of these dimensions and work through these questions. Notice the manifestations of these behaviors as much as you can over the next week and come back to the questions to add your observations and insight.

* **Action**  
  * Notice your mind's primary response to negative emotion.   
  * What will happen if you do what your mind is telling you to do?  
  * What does it feel like to not try to give into it?  
  * What will happen if you do nothing about the situation?  
  * Notice your feeling of control over the situation before and after you take action.   
  * What makes it important to be in control of your external situation?   
  * What makes it challenging to be in control of your internal environment?  
* **Anger**  
  * Write about 2-3 situations in which you get angry.  
  * Do you have control in that situation?  
  * How do you feel about yourself?  
  * What does your mind tell you about what needs to happen for your anger to go away? What is the “solution” to your emotion? What does the anger tell you to do?  
  * Assume that it is giving you a solution \- what is the problem that it is arising in response to? What makes it a problem?  
  * Notice the action of your ego. Are your thoughts about the internal world or the external world?  
* **Impulsive Behavior**  
  * When is it difficult to resist impulses?  
  * When is it easy to resist impulses?  
  * List the impulses that are easier to resist than others.  
  * Pick an impulse that you struggle with, and try to delay it by 5 minutes before you give into it.  
    * What physical sensations or emotions do you experience:  
      * 5 minutes before you give into the impulse  
      * While you are giving into the impulse.  
      * 10 minutes after you have given into the impulse.  
      * 1 hour after you have given into the impulse.  
    * When you can no longer resist the impulse, try to maintain awareness of your thoughts, feelings and physical sensations as you give into it.  
    * What changes between when you pay attention and when you don’t?  
* **Non-help seeking behavior**  
  * Think of a task that you find difficult  
    * What sensations or emotions do you feel when you think about getting help?  
    * How would it feel to receive help without asking for it?  
    * How would you feel If you accomplished something meaningful while getting help?  
    * How would it feel to be dependant on someone?  
    * What would be the easiest thing to ask for help with? What would be the hardest?
