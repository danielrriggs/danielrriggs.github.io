# **The Weird Stuff Pt. 1**

*November 25, 2024*

## **Outline:**

- Explore the ordered systems or principles behind spiritual deeds, such as charity, kindness, and sacrifice.  
- Recognize a common pitfall in religious or spiritual traditions: mimicry. Many attempt to imitate the actions of enlightened beings without undertaking the internal transformation necessary for authentic change.  
- Understand the concept of shakti, a spiritual energy associated with the primal feminine force.  
  - Realize that not all actions yield the same results; outcomes vary based on the accumulation and quality of one’s shakti.  
  - Learn to accumulate shakti through “internal” renunciation—releasing attachments within rather than giving up material possessions.  
- “Shortcuts” to accumulate shakti: devote oneself *fully* to actions  
  - Reframe meditation, spirituality, and spiritual practice. Recognize meditation caveats  
  - In tantra, specific practices and paths are designed to connect directly to the source of shakti.

### Medium Quest – Practice Internal Renunciation

*Shakti is the energy cultivated when we sacrifice something deeply desired without expectation of personal gain. Often, people think they are making sacrifices when in reality, they’re feeding underlying desires. In this exercise, identify an attachment or strong desire, then practice releasing any attachment to control or outcomes associated with it. This internal “giving up” is a way to cultivate authentic shakti.*

**Activity**– fill out the table below with the following steps, and **for the next 3 days, practice the renunciation statements you write for yourself** for internal renunciation

1. **List 3-5 Attachments or Desires:**   
   Write down three attachments or desires that occupy your thoughts or emotions, such as a material possession, goal, or relationship.

2. **Identify Ego Thoughts:**  
   Examine if there are ego thoughts tied to each attachment and consider why it’s challenging to let go.

3. **Create Renunciation Statements:**  
   Prepare statements that alter ego thoughts toward neutrality or acceptance. These statements should emphasize actions rather than specific outcomes.

4. **Practice and Reflect:**  
   Observe any resistance throughout the day and take notes on how it affects your feelings or mindset.

### Advanced Quest \- The Devotion Challenge

*True accumulation of shakti arises from performing an action fully devoted to the act itself, without any expectation of gain or reward. This quest challenges you to reframe your understanding of meditation and spiritual practice by bringing your fullest dedication as if each meditation session were a grand final.*

**Activity– For the next 5 days, practice a meditation of your choice with the intentions you write for yourself from the intention table below.** 

1. **Choose a Meditation** that suits your temperament  
   * If you're uncertain, try Nadi Shuddhi Pranayama Meditation (Follow along with Dr. K: [10 Minute Daily Meditation](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1W7oUZ8EVbI))

2. *(use below table)* **Identity opportunities to accumulate shakti**  
   * Reflect on moments of perceived weakness or failure, such as procrastination, self-doubt, or impatience.  
   * Reframe each as an opportunity for growth and shakti accumulation.

3. *(use below table)* **Set intention statements for your meditation practice**   
   * Use the identified opportunities to form intention statements that reflect how you’ll use these moments to grow.

4. **Set Pre-Meditation Intention**:  
   * Before each session, review and repeat each intention three times, either quietly but verbally or out loud on your mind, to fully commit.

5. **Meditate with Full Presence**:  
   * Let go of all outcomes during meditation. Dedicate 100% of your energy to simply being present in the experience.
