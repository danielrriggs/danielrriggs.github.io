# **The Roots of Tiredness**

*January 30, 2025*

## **Outline:**

- Understand that the level of energy and the feeling of tiredness are two different concepts  
- Tiredness can be understood through the lens of the three gunas: Tamas (inertia), Rajas (activity), and Sattva (balance). An imbalance in these energies can lead to feelings of tiredness.  
- Tiredness can be viewed as a result of past karmas, with current actions determining future energy levels. Pay attention to the connection between overall energy and daily choices like diet, sleep, and exercise  
- Differentiate different sources of tiredness:  
  - Tiredness can be a signal for energy conservation due to psychological factors like ego  
  - Tiredness can result from over-expenditure of energy. This can occur when someone engages in excessive activity, leading to burnout  
  - Actions motivated by negative emotions help us return only to a baseline state
