## **Weekly Quest**

This quest explores how your past and present relationships shape your attachment style and provide tools for healing and growth. 

**Objective:**  To explore your attachment style, understand its origins, and develop strategies for healing and growth.

**Lite Quests:**

**Reflecting on Past Relationships**

**Activity:** Think about a significant past relationship (romantic, friendship, or family). Write a brief description of this relationship and how it ended. Reflect on and list any lingering feelings or thoughts you have about this relationship. Discuss how this relationship may have influenced your current attachment style (e.g., anxious, avoidant, secure).

| Relationship Type | Description | Ending | Lingering Feelings/Thoughts | Influence on Current Attachment Style |
| ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- |
| *Romantic* | *College relationship that lasted 2 years* | *Ended due to moving to different cities* | *Sadness, occasional nostalgia* | *Tend to feel anxious about long-distance relationships* |

**Reflection**: 

* How do these past relationships continue to impact your current relationships and attachment style?   
* What insights have you gained about your attachment behavior?

**Identifying Triggers and Responses**

**Activity:** List three common situations or events that trigger strong emotional reactions for you. For each trigger, describe your internal experiences (thoughts and feelings). Outline your typical mental and behavioral responses to these triggers.

| Trigger Situation/ Event | Internal Experiences (Thoughts/Feelings) | Mental Response | Behavioral Response |
| ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- |
| *Being ignored at a social event* | *Feeling unimportant, rejected* | *Negative self-talk* | *Withdrawal from social interaction* |

**Reflection:** 

* How do these triggers affect your daily life and relationships?   
* What patterns do you notice in your responses to these triggers?   
* How can you reinterpret these triggers to respond more positively in the future?   
* ***Challenge:** Share one example with a partner or small group of friends and discuss alternative ways to interpret and cope with the trigger.*

**Medium Quest:**

**Family Dynamics Exploration**

**Activity:** Answer the following questions about your family:

* Who would you say raised you?  
* Did you see your grandparents when you were little?  
* Did you have other people living in the house?  
* How did your parents treat your siblings?  
* List adjectives/words to describe your relationship with your siblings.  
* List five adjectives/words to describe your relationship with your mother.  
* List five adjectives/words to describe your relationship with your father.  
* Which caregiver do you feel closest to?

**Reflection:** 

* How have your family dynamics shaped your  attachment style?   
* What positive influences do you receive from your family?   
* What negative influences or challenges did you face?   
* How can you use this understanding to improve your current relationships? 

**Coping Strategy Development**

**Activity:** Choose one of the triggers you identified in the previous exercise. Reflect on the internal experience associated with this trigger. Develop a coping strategy for managing this internal experience, such as mindfulness techniques, positive affirmations, or practical actions.

| Trigger | Internal Experiences | Coping Strategy |
| ----- | ----- | ----- |
| *Being ignored at a social event* | *Feeling unimportant, rejected* | *Practice positive affirmations, focus on engaging with another person* |

**Reflection:** 

* How effective is this coping strategy in managing your emotional responses?   
* What changes do you notice in your behavior when you use this strategy?   
* How can you further refine this strategy to make it more effective? 

**Advanced Quest:**

**Metabolizing Past Relationships**

**Activity:** Write a letter to a person from a past significant relationship (you don’t need to send it). In the letter, express any unresolved feelings or thoughts you have about the relationship. Highlight how this relationship has shaped your attachment style and personal growth.

**Reflection:** 

* What emotions arose while writing this letter?   
* How has this process helped you understand your attachment style better?   
* What steps can you take to continue healing and growing form this past relationship?
