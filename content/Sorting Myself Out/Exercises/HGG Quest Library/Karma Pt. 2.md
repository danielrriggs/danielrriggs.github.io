#### **Advanced Quest: Introspection**

**Think of a negative situation in your life right now. What makes it negative?**

* What were the causes that had to have happened to lead to this situation? Try NOT to use logic to answer this question for yourself.  
* What power do you have over this situation? Consider other things you could do that you are not thinking of because you are not willing to bear the consequences.
