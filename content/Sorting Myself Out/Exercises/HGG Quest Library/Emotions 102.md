## **Weekly Quest**

This quest builds on the concepts introduced in Emotions 101 by delving into complex emotional experiences and their impact on our lives. 

**Objective:**  To explore how individuals can better manage their emotional landscape through various exercises and meditations. 

**Lite Quests:**

**Emotion Identification Practice**

**Activity:** Spend 5-10 minutes daily identifying and naming your emotions. Use the emotional wheel to identify feelings you are having a hard time recognizing your emotions. Track these emotions and note any patterns or recurring feelings.   
*Note: For an extra challenge, track at the beginning and end of each day.*

| Date/Time | Emotion | Secondary Emotion | Where in your body do you feel this?  | Did a thought or event trigger this emotion? |
| ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- |
| *Aug 21st, 8am*  | *Anxious* | *Overwhelmed* | *Tightness in chest and throat* | *Upcoming presentation at work/school* |

**Trigger Awareness: Identifying Triggers and Responses**

**Activity:** During the week, write down three situations or events that trigger strong emotional reactions for you. For each trigger, describe your internal experiences (thoughts and feelings). Outline your typical mental and behavioral responses to these triggers.

| Trigger Situation/ Event | Internal Experiences (Thoughts/Feelings) | Mental Response | Behavioral Response |
| ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- |
| *Being ignored at a social event* | *Feeling unimportant, rejected* | *Negative self-talk* | *Withdrawal from social interaction* |

**Medium Quests:**

**Mixed Emotion Exploration**

**Activity:** Reflect on a recent experience where you felt multiple emotions simultaneously. For example, you might have felt both happy and anxious at a family gathering. Write about how each emotion influenced your behavior and thought process. 

Consider questions like: 

* Did one emotion overpower the other?   
* Did they coexist peacefully, or did they create internal conflict?

**Example:**  
*“During last week's team meeting, I felt both proud and nervous when presenting my ideas. Pride gave me the confidence to speak up, but the nervousness made me rush through my points. In the future, I might focus on grounding myself in the moment to balance these emotions better.”*

**Mixed Emotion Exploration**

**Alternate Nostril Breathing (Nadi Shodhana)**

**Activity:** Nadi Shodhana is a breathing technique that balances the mind and emotions by regulating the flow of breath through the nostrils. It can help to calm the nervous system, reduce stress, and bring a sense of clarity and focus. Practice Nadi Shodhana for 15 minutes a day, followed by a 10 \- 15 min meditation bringing awareness to the change in your mind and emotions. 

* Sit comfortably with your spine straight.   
* Close your right nostril with your right thumb.   
* Inhale deeply through your left nostril, then close your left nostril with your right ring finger.  
* Open your right nostril and exhale through the right nostril. Inhale through the right nostril, close it, and exhale through the left nostril.   
* Continue alternating for 5-10 minutes ending with exhaling complete through your left nostril.

**Emotional Resilience Building**

**Activity:** Choose a challenging situation in your life—such as a conflict at work, a personal disappointment, or a stressful event—and apply one or more techniques from the lecture to build emotional resilience. Techniques might include mindfulness, reframing negative thoughts, or practicing gratitude. Document your process, challenges, and outcomes.

* Situation  
* Resilience Technique  
* Challenges with the technique  
* Benefits with the technique  
* Outcome of the situation  
* Reflection on the process

**Example:** 

* ***Situation:** Conflict with a coworker*  
* ***Resilience Technique:** Mindfulness*   
* ***Challenges with the technique:** It was difficult to stay present during the conflict. Trying to listen to what was being said and not get caught up in my own head was challenging.*   
* ***Benefits with the technique**: I did remain calmer and less reactive than I have in the past.*   
* ***Outcome of the situation:** Was able to resolve the issue calmly and the relationship improved.*   
* ***Reflection on the process:** Being present is helpful and will take some practice.* 


  
**Understanding the Brahma Viharas**

**Activity:** Explore the Four Brahma Viharas (Divine Abodes) and identify how the Near and Far Enemies affect the cultivation of these qualities in daily life. 

1. Reflect on a recent situation where you tried to practice Loving-Kindness, Compassion, Joy, or Equanimity.   
2. Identify if you encountered the New Enemy (e.g., sentimentality for Loving-Kindness, pity for Compassion) or the Far Enemy (e.g., malice for Loving-Kindness, cruelty for Compassion).   
3. Write a brief reflection on how these “enemies” influenced your emotions.   
4. Write a brief reflection on how you can better cultivate the Brahma Viharas. 

| Scenario | Identify the Near/Far Enemy | Relect on how these influence your emotions | Reflect on how to better cultivate Brahma Viharas |
| ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- |
| *Feeling pity for a struggling team member* | *Pity (for Compassion)* | *Felt superior instead of truly compassionate* | *Focus on empathy and understanding rather than superiority* |
| *Jealousy over a friend’s good fortune* | *Jealousy (for Joy)* | *Caused resentment instead of happiness for them* | *Remind myself of the challenges they’ve overcome and genuinely celebrate their success* |
