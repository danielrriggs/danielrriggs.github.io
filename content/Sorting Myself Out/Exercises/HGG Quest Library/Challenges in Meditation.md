## **Weekly Quest**

This weekly quest guides you through the basics of starting and maintaining a meditation routine. 

**Objective:**  To explore and create a regular meditation practice and learn how to make meditation a part of your regular routine.

**Practicing with the Breath**

**Activity:** Box Breathing or Nadi Shodna for 5-10 mins a day. 

Equal Breathing/Box Breathing (Sama Vritti)

* Sit comfortably with your spine straight.   
* Close your eyes and take a deep breath in through your nose, counting to four.   
* Exhale through your nose, also counting to four.   
* Maintain an equal duration for inhales and exhales.   
* Gradually increase the count to six or eight as you become more comfortable. Practice for 5-10 minutes.

Alternate Nostril Breathing (Nadi Shodhana)

* Sit comfortably with your spine straight.   
* Close your right nostril with your right thumb.   
* Inhale deeply through your left nostril, then close your left nostril with your right ring finger.  
* Open your right nostril and exhale through the right nostril. Inhale through the right nostril, close it, and exhale through the left nostril.   
* Continue alternating for 5-10 minutes ending with exhaling complete through your left nostril. 

#### **Medium Quest:** 

**Pratyahara and Dharana**

Activity: Spend 20-30 minutes each day practicing Pratyahara (withdrawal of senses) followed by Dharana (concentration) for 20 minutes. 

* Start by finding a quiet place to sit comfortably.   
* Focus on drawing your attention inward, away from external distractions.   
  * You can focus on your breath trying to block out any external sounds  
  * You can imagine you are slowly withdrawing your senses one by one. Visualize each sense being turned inward.   
* Then, concentrate on a single point (e.g., Trataka candle flame) or a mantra (Aum, So Hum, Om Shanti, etc.).  
  * Sit in a dark room with a candle placed about three feet in front of you. Gaze at the flame until your eyes water.  
  * Close your eyes and see the after-image of the flame in the dark space behind your eyelids (*chidakasha)* for as long as the image remains clear. Try to hold it there. Notice the correlation between your effort and the persistence of the image.  
  * Build a regular practice, increasing the duration of visualization as your concentration improves. Never continue if you experience pain.

**Reflection:** What challenges did you face during Pratyahara and Dharana? How did these practices affect your ability to concentrate and withdraw from sensory distractions?

**Mind-Body Integration**

**Activity:** Before your pratyahara and dharana practice, engage in a 30-45-minute session of yoga or Tai Chi. Focus on the movements and their connection with your breath. Maintain mindfulness throughout the practice.

Notice how your meditation practice changes by doing yoga or tai chi before hand. 

**Reflection:** How did the practice of yoga or Tai Chi influence your mind-body awareness? What did you notice in your overall well-being or your next meditation practice? 

#### **Advanced Quest: ** 

**Activity:** Build a structured morning routine for you to practice meditation.   
Your routine should include: 

* Preparatory practice for the body (Yoga, tai chi) for about 15 minutes or approximately 3 rounds or Surya Namaskara  
* Pranayama (with mudra) for 5-10 minutes  
* Mantra chanting for 5-10 minutes  
* Dharana of your choice for 20 minutes  
* Wait before eating for your body to absorb the practice  
* Eat a sattvic breakfast

Example Structured Morning Routine

* Activity: Follow this morning routine each day:  
* Wake up between 4:30 \- 5:00 am.  
* Perform 3 rounds of Surya Namaskara (sun salutation).  
* Practice Nadi Shodhana (alternate nostril breathing) for 6 minutes, accompanied by Jnana Mudra (gesture of knowledge).  
* Chant Aum (Om) for a few minutes.  
* Engage in Dharana of your choice:  
  * Trataka (gazing at a fixed point, such as a candle flame) combined with Khechari Mudra (tongue lock).  
  * Ajna Chakra Sadhana (focusing on the third eye) combined with Chin Mudra (gesture of consciousness).  
* Wait 1 hour before eating a sattvic (pure and balanced) breakfast.

**Reflection:** How did following this structured routine affect your day? What benefits or challenges did you experience with early rising and this sequence of practices?

***For extra benefits, add a second 20-30 meditation practice to the end of your day.***
