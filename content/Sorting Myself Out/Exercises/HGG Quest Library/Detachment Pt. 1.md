## **Outline:**

* Detachment comes from the Sanskrit “Vairagya”  
  * Vairagya comes from the Sanskrit root “vi,” – which means without, and rag, which means “passion, interest, attachment.”  
  * So detachment means to be without interest, passion, or attachment.   
* What is the difference between dissociation and detachment  
* The flow state \-\> stop being ourselves and merge with the work at hand  
* What is apathy  
* True detachment is having internal tranquility and recognizing that the world around you is separate from you.  
  * What you can control  
  * How to cultivate this attitude  
* Future focus vs. present focus and karma phal  
  * Devoting yourself to action, not the outcome  
* Tat Tvam Asi \- meditation practice


## **Weekly Quest**

**Objective:** To practice detachment in everyday situations by improving self-awareness and detachment.

#### **Lite Quests:** 

**Dancing in the moment:**   
***Easy:***  
Find a private space, put on your favorite music, and dance freely for at least 10 minutes. Focus on the movement and the music, letting go of any judgments or expectations.   
***Hard:***  
Dance publicly\!

**Mindfulness Meditation:**   
Spend 10 minutes each day practicing mindfulness meditation. Focus on your breath and observe any thoughts without attachment, allowing them to pass like clouds in the sky.

#### **Medium Quest:** 

**Detachment in Action:**

Identify three situations where you typically feel attached. How can you approach these situations with detachment? Consider the potential benefits.

| Situation | Typical Reaction | Detached Approach | Benefits |
| ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- |
| *Work Conflict* | *Feeling agitated and taking things personally* | *“It was a disagreement.”* | *Reduce stress, clearer problem solving* |
| *Overdue project deadlines* | *Stress and anxiety over timelines* | *Step back and make a determination on if the deadline is realistic, needs to be reworked, and what needs to happen to achieve it.* | *Enhanced focus, better quality work* |
| *Social Media Scrolling* | *Wasting time and emotional energy* | *Analyze the value of your time spent on social media.* | *Improved emotional regulation and more contentment.* |

#### **Advanced Quest: ** 

Shoonya Meditation

For a week, practice Shoonya meditation twice a day for 10-15 minutes each time.   
Shoonya Meditation Steps: 

* Find a quiet place where you won’t be disturbed.  
* Sit in a comfortable position with your back straight.  
* Close your eyes and take a few deep breaths.  
* Bring your attention to the space between your thoughts.  
* As thoughts come into your mind, simply observe them and let them go.  
* Focus on the space between your thoughts for as long as you like.  
* When you’re ready, open your eyes and take a few deep breaths.

**Karma Phal Contemplation**

#### For one week, note down actions you take and detach yourself from the results. Reflect each evening on how it felt to act without attachment to the outcome. Consider how this practice may influence your understanding of karma phal.

| Action Taken | Expected Outcome | Detached Perspective | Insights Gained |
| ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- |
| *Offered help to a colleague* | *They would feel grateful and reciprocate* | *They needed help and I could help, so I did what I could.* | *Found joy in the act of giving itself, regardless of reciprocation* |
| *Applied for a job* | *Expectation to get an interview* | *Applied with best effort, left the result to unfold naturally* | *Felt less anxious and more open to other opportunities* |

#### As you practice detaching from the results of your actions, keep an eye on how your ego reacts. When things go well or not, does it feel like it’s all about you? Try to spot any automatic habits in how you react—those are your vasanas at play. Also, notice when you get really attached to outcomes or really want to avoid them—those feelings are called kleshas.

#### Each day, think about these questions:

* #### How does your ego feel when things turn out differently than you wanted?

* #### What habits are steering your feelings and actions when you’re not paying attention?

* #### Are there moments when your likes and dislikes might be clouding your judgment?

#### Remember, you’re not trying to judge yourself or make immediate changes. You're learning to be aware of these patterns as they happen.
