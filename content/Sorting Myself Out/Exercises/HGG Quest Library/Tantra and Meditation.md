## **Weekly Quest**

Tantra is about harnessing energy through rigorous practice and commitment. Here are some exercises to help enhance self-awareness as you observe energy shifts in your body and mind.   
Try to do at least one of these practices twice a day for the next two weeks, and notice how you feel before and after—Journal about the difference. 

**Objective:** To practice purification techniques so that you can refine your perception and prepare yourself to practice tantra. Pay attention to the nuances of your experiences before and after each session.

**Yoga Practice**  
Begin each day with a practice of grounding in the material/physical layer of reality. Focus on anchoring yourself in the present moment and feeling your body's movement and energy. 

Practice Surya Namaskara every day for at least a week after you wake up. You can learn it from this 2020 webinar here: [https://youtu.be/Cb3BXbWKDv4?si=zmTsWYEZMYyptFMD\&t=84](https://youtu.be/Cb3BXbWKDv4?si=zmTsWYEZMYyptFMD&t=84)

**Yoga Journal Prompts:** 

1. As you moved through the Sun Salutations, what internal or external resistances did you encounter during your practices? How did you feel before the practice, and how do you feel afterward?

2. If you could describe your internal energy, what would it be before and after your practices, and why?

**Overall Weekly Practice Journal Prompt**

1. What would your life look like 1 year from now, 2 years from now, 5 years from now if you were to keep doing these practices and acting from purified state of mind?
