# **Deep Dive into Anger**

*June 11, 2025*

# Outline

### Anger as Emotional Armor

- Anger is rooted in the **amygdala** and **limbic system**, which process threat and emotion.  
- **Amygdala activation** can result in either fear or anger—what differentiates them is **ego presence**.  
- **Rajasic ego (ahamkara)** \+ amygdala activation \= anger.  
- Anger wipes the mental slate clean—**context and logic vanish** for the sake of immediate action.  
- This is **evolutionarily adaptive**, but problematic in modern society where context matters.

### Transmuting Anger

- **Return context** and practice **ego awareness** to calm the reactive mind.  
- Anger can fuel **growth and right action** when channeled skillfully.  
- Use anger to **advocate**, not to attack. Calm \+ conviction works better than force.  
- **Spiritual traditions** recognize anger as a **potential source of samadhi**—intense focus and clarity.  
- Even divine figures like **Shiva** display anger—what matters is how it is **used**.

# Quest

## Light Quest – Uncover the Shadow Emotion

*Notice when your emotional state doesn’t quite match your reality—this is often a clue for deeper, repressed emotions like anger. Oftentimes when anger is repressed, it shows up as sadness / shame / numbness.* 

**Activity Steps:**

1. **Identify an emotion that’s been persistent this week** (e.g., loneliness, hopelessness, touch starvation, guilt).  
2. Ask yourself:  
   * “If my life only existed for the next hour, would this emotion still feel so strong?”  
   * “Is this emotional reaction **disproportionate** to my current moment?”  
3. **If yes—write it down. This might be a clue that something deeper (like repressed anger) is underneath.**

## Medium Quest – Trace the Blame

*If you’re harsh on yourself, trace who you’re protecting or refusing to blame. This exercise is about unburdening, not blaming permanently. Therefore, be honest and/or creative—even if the answer feels wrong or forbidden.*

**Activity Steps:**

1. Think of a recent moment of **self-blame, guilt, or emotional flatness / depression.**  
2. **Journal your inner narrative:**   
   1. What happened?   
   2. Why do you think it’s your fault?  
3. **Ask yourself:**  
   1. “Who might also be responsible here that I don’t want to blame?”  
   2. “Who did I protect by blaming myself?”  
4. **Reflect afterwards:**   
   1. What truth did I not want to admit?  
   2. How would my inner world shift if I allowed myself to name this?

I disappear when people need me.* | *My friend never told me they were struggling and tends to drop hints instead of being direct. Then afterwards he always told me he felt disappointed at me.* | *I’ve taken all the emotional responsibility. I’m angry that I’ve wanted to support but I don’t know how to and always get blamed.Naming their lack of openness lets me stop over-owning everything.* |

**Example Anahata Meditation \- Bhramari Pranayama (Humming Bee Breath)**

1. Sit comfortably. Close your eyes and take a few deep breaths.  
2. Position your fingers:  
* Thumbs gently press over your ear flaps.  
* Index fingers rest on the outer corners of your eyes.  
* Middle fingers on inner eye corners.  
* Ring fingers on either side of the nostrils.  
* Pinky fingers on the crease where your lips meet.  
3. Inhale deeply through the nose.  
4. On the exhale, make a soft humming sound like a bee.  
5. Focus on the **vibration in your chest and face**, especially your heart center.  
6. Repeat for 5–10 rounds or until your timer ends.

**Bonus:** After the practice, sit quietly and *just listen*—to sounds around you and within.
