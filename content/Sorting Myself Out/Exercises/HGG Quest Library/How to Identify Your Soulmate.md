# **How to Identify Your Soulmate**

*March 03, 2025*

## **Outline:**

- Define “soulmates” through different types of love, including Eros Love (romantic passion), Storge Love (deep friendship), Mania Love (obsessive love) and Pragma Love (practical love).  
- Explore the Rate of Change in Intimacy Model and Self-Expansion Model to understand how intimacy develops and how to sustain passion in relationships.  
- Understand how dating apps impact soulmate connections, as they often prioritize practical compatibility (Pragma) over emotional chemistry (Eros).  
- Recognize soulmates as karmic connections—relationships that transcend logic and emotions, often feeling destined or deeply transformative.  
- Practical Steps to Finding a Soulmate:  
  - Increase your exposure by meeting and interacting with as many people as possible.  
  - Shift away from purely logical decision-making—stay open, flexible, and allow connections to develop naturally.

## **Quests:**

### Light Quest – Are They My Soulmate or Just Filling a Void?

*Objective: Differentiate between true soulmate connections and relationships that feel fulfilling simply because they meet an emotional deficit.*

**Activity – Over the next 5 days, reflect on past or present romantic interests and analyze whether your attraction stems from deep compatibility or an emotional void being filled.**

**Steps:**

1. **List Romantic Interests:** Write down past or present people you’ve had strong romantic feelings for.

2. **Identify the Emotional Needs They Filled:** Reflect on how they made you feel and what emotional gaps they helped fill.

3. **Examine If You Would Feel the Same Without That Need:** Imagine you no longer had that emotional void—would you still be drawn to them?

4. **Write Down Your Insights:** What realizations do you have after this analysis? Were they truly your soulmate, or were they fulfilling an unmet need?

**Bonus Reflection Questions:**

* Were they your primary (or only) source of emotional support?

*By identifying patterns in your romantic attractions, you can make more conscious relationship choices and recognize when you're seeking a soulmate versus filling a void.*

### Medium Quest – Cultivating Passion Through Balanced Sharings

***Objective:** Learn how to build and maintain passion in relationships by pacing emotional intimacy. Avoid overwhelming potential partners by gradually deepening emotional connections instead of rushing or withholding too much.*

**Activity: For the next week, reflect on past conversations where you shared something personal.** 

**Steps:**

1. **Recall past conversations** where you shared something meaningful with someone new.  
2. **Analyze how the other person reacted**—did they lean in or pull away?  
3. **Evaluate if the conversation felt balanced** or if it moved too quickly/slowly.  
4. **Reflect** on how you could have paced the sharing better.  
5. **Apply** these insights to future interactions.

| *Chat with coworker*  | *Opened up about feeling stuck in my career.*  | *They listened and shared similar struggles.*  | *It felt balanced.*  | *This was a good example of gradual intimacy.*  |

Do you have a healthy outlet for processing emotions outside of romantic connections?

### Advanced Quest – Opening Doors for Connections and Cultivating Sparks

*Objective: Take intentional actions to create opportunities for meaningful connections, rather than passively waiting for a soulmate to appear. Shift your mindset from judgment and analysis to openness, allowing chemistry and connection to develop naturally.*

**Activity: For the next week, identify and engage in activities that increase opportunities for connection. Practice letting go of instant judgments about potential partners or friends.**

**Steps:**

1. **List out activities or events that can help you meet new people:** This could mean attending social events, engaging in hobbies outside your home, or making small changes in daily interactions (e.g., talking to more people at work or your regular coffee shop).  
2. **Write Down Initial Judgments** you tend to make about people you meet.  
3. **Identify the Judgement's Effects:** how did the judgement affect the interaction or how could it have affected the interaction?  
4. **Observe what changes when you let go of judgment** and focus on enjoying the interaction.
