## **Weekly Quest**

We examined discipline as a complex internal process rather than just a series of external actions. True discipleship stems from one-pointed focus of the mind, also known as Dharana, and is influenced by our environment, attachments, and values. That is why this quest aims to help you explore your personal internal discipline. 

**Objective:** To identify your motivations, form new habits, or temporarily give up certain activities/habits to deepen your practice and understanding. 

### **Lite Quests**

**Daily Dharna Meditation**  
Each day, dedicate 5-10 minutes on a focus exercise, such as Trataka (candle gazing), to practice your concentration. Can do this any time of day. 

**Motivational 4 Quadrants**  
Create a four-quadrant chart to map your personal motivations. As you place your motivations down, really dig into and unearth the values driving them. Try to notice what is being driven by a “should” or a desire. 

| Motivation(Action/Task/Duty) | Where is it on the quadrant | List Value(s) | List Should(s) | List desire(s) |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| To work out every day |  | Health | I should work out because otherwise, people will think I’m lazy. I need to work out because I’ve gained weight.  | I want people to think I’m attractive |

* As you perform your task/duty/motivation, note how perceiving a task as a duty vs a desire changes your approach to doing the action. 

### **Medium Quest**

**Habit Formation**  
Pick the easiest habit you can think of. Now, make it 50% easier.   
Commit to doing that 50% habit every day for the next week/2 weeks. 

| Habit | 50% easier | Trigger to do habit |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Floss my teeth | Floss a tooth | After I brush my teeth |

Note: Notice how varying emotions influence your discipline of your habit each day. If you resisted desire, reflect on your feelings during the resistance, right after, and later on. If you did not resist the desire, reflect on your feelings during the desire, right after, and later on. What could you do differently next time? 

### **Advanced Quest**

**Anushtanam**  
Option A: Choose a favorite item, food, or activity, and abstain from it for the next 40 days.  
Option B: Do the following exercises for 40 days

* 3 rounds of surya namskara  
* 9 breaths of Kapal Bhatti  
* 9 rounds of Nadi Shodhana  
* 108 malas in 40 days   
  * 2 per day on average  
  * 7 per day once a week  
  * mantra or aum changing  
* Antar Trataka
