Visualization exercise.

###### Introduction
People who have a mirror in their room usually tend to keep their room cleaner. And people who have a mirror also tend to appear better. And the reason is because they look at themselves every day. That's all.

Get naked and look at yourself in the mirror every day & understand that you have to do something about your body.

Just like that, this exercise is a mind mirror.

You are unconsciously an amalgamation of three people - who you were, who you are, and who you want to be.

This is a time for a tall glass of honesty.

###### Exercise
If you're strong with visualization, you can just visualize this; if not then draw it (on paper).

Ask yourself how you feel about any goal that you have. If you don't have any goals, how do you feel about your future?

Now ask yourself, Is that how I want to be? Emotionally. Do I want my emotional body to look like this?

This uses a function of the brain called synesthesia which converts feelings into a visual. This is what all artists use. They take the feelings and they paint it. We want to do the same thing. Take the feelings and draw them.

When you look at this image, either in your mind or on paper in front of you, what would you change about this?

And just change it.

Those of you who are strong with visualization can just visualize it. And those of you who are not strong with visualization, just redraw it.

When you start making a less distorted and more beautiful body, You'll automatically feel better because by synesthesia, Your brain converts that back into a feeling. And then you understand, I want to feel like this all the time. I don't want to get distorted.

How do you establish that you don't get distorted again by the samskara? You keep drawing. Every day, at least three times. For the next nine days. That's all you need to do. And in nine days, you would have drawn yourself 27 times.

When my guru asked me to do this  I did not like who I was at that point. And I said, This is a distorted image of a person. I would like to be like this. And then he said, Yes, go ahead and be that. Never forget who you are. Who you want to be. And who you were. And don't distort it. If you are exactly who you are right now, you have an accurate assessment of your power.  It may not be flattering if your ego is big. And if you're suffering from low self-esteem, it's probably because you haven't really assessed who you are. And then you'll know what you have to work on. Instead of just feeling miserable about yourself, you'll know what you've got to work on.
