From: [[The Fastest Way to Health, Paul Chek]]

Dr. Happiness

- 1) What is happy making for me?
- 2) What is my overarching dream? How do I choose to earn a living & life?
- 3) What are my current goals to accomplish my dream?
	- Set goals if you don't have them
- 4) Have clearly defined "core values" to guide my choices (yes/no)

Pay attention to how you feel when you answer these questions. Don't let your shadow get in the way.
