[You are a 25 Year Old Loner - YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=J4M5Den94M0)

Society's influence on loneliness and isolation is often overlooked, and understanding this perspective is crucial in finding a path forward for 25 year old loners.

---

Take a karmic perspective and focus on what you can do today to achieve success, instead of what you should do.

---

Your self-worth does not have to be determined by the responses of others, focus on your actions and what you can control.

###### What is it like to be a 20-something loner? Or a 30-something loner? What emotions does it bring up? What is your experience of being a “loner”? List all the experiences you can recall that caused this identity to form within you.

[Why You Feel Desperate and Lonely](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rOi-SrJB6vI)

If you come off as desperate, depressed, angry, or view people of other genders as just reproductive organs, you'll likely keep incurring the same dating problems.

---

Be careful about drawing your own conclusions about why other people don’t want to date you. Seek real feedback from the people who reject you, and avoid acting on your own personal biases.

###### Sit with the feeling of desperation. What does it feel like? Where does it reside in your body? Is it similar to any other physical sensations that you experience?

[Psychiatrist on solving "I lack human connection"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7khssDVKKXc)

Overcoming the fear of rejection and showing our true selves to others is the only way to break free from the cycle of insecurity and lack of connection.

---

Connecting with others involves being vulnerable and paying attention to other people.

###### What makes it difficult to connect with other human beings? How do you feel about yourself before, during, and after a social interaction? Is it hard to reach out to others? Why or why not? What thoughts does your mind throw at you? On a scale of 1 to 10, (1 being totally fake, and 10 being very real), how real do these thoughts _feel_?

[Therapist Reacts: "I am too boring for other people."](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zh9r78Wd0kI)

The belief that "I'm a bad person" can lead to non-traditional narcissism and idealizing others, causing a disconnect in friendships.

---

Keep an eye out for when your ahamkara (ego) becomes active, as it leads to difficulty in connecting with other people.

###### Think of the most interesting person that you have met? What made them an interesting person? What would you have to take away from them to make them a boring person? Are they objectively a boring or an interesting person? Who doesn’t found them interesting? Why? How do you think your life would change if you became interesting? If you had to give another person advice on becoming more interesting to you, what would you tell them?

[A Perspective on Female Loneliness](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UbGZaGzWdfs)

Loneliness can be a paradoxical challenge for women who have advantages that others crave, but don't understand the difficulties that come with it.

---

Invalidating someone's struggle with loneliness can be harmful and make it even harder for them to connect with others.

---

The impact of trauma, abuse, and bullying can lead to social anxiety and extreme loneliness, making it difficult to form meaningful relationships.

###### Loneliness is about feeling disconnected from other people. Think about the experience of the person in this video. What similarities do you share with them? What differentiates you from them? What do you think they would like to hear? What do you think you would like to hear?

[How To Not Be Awkward - YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oTrCLOyoRq8)

Trying not to be awkward actually makes you more awkward, as it shifts your attention to your mind and away from the present moment.

---

It's important to remember that a conversation involves two people, and if someone else doesn't know how to engage in a dialogue, it's not the socially anxious person's fault.

---

Socially anxious people often struggle in social interactions because they may have grown up with oblivious communicators, making it important to find others who are attuned to them.

###### What happens when you engage in or think of engaging in a social interaction? What kinds of thoughts do you have about what will happen when you interact with others? Write down as many thoughts as you can about potential consequences. Then, step back and read those thoughts as if they came from another person with completely different circumstances. What would you say to the other person?

[Stop Letting Social Anxiety Control You](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YiUSQzV1Aqo)

Humans struggle with social anxiety because they don't use the same circuits as animals, relying on analysis and tools instead.

---

Animals use different circuits in their brain to overcome social anxiety, while humans can learn to alleviate it by utilizing body language, tone, and other senses.

---

Practice social skills like eye contact, smiling, and simple greetings for 10-15 seconds to overcome social anxiety and improve social interactions.

###### Think of the last social interaction you had where you were interacting with more than 2 people. In hindsight, would you classify the interaction as positive or negative? In the moment, how would you have thought the interaction was going? Would you call it positive or negative? Can you recall the last “neutral” social interaction you had? What would have to happen for an interaction to be neutral?

[Loneliness - The Silent Struggle We All Feel](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dWS3A2EAwTk)

Loneliness cannot be solved independently, society needs to be more tolerant and recognize the importance of human connection.

---

Focus on helping others and engaging in service to overcome loneliness and conquer negative emotions.

---

Reach out to three people for THEIR sake, not yours, and and let's be more understanding and helpful towards each other.

###### Think of 3 people that you haven’t thought of in a long time. Who are the first three people that come to mind? Now think about reaching out to them. What comes to mind and what do you feel? Write a few lines about each of these people and how they will react when you reach out to them.

[LONELINESS: The Danger of Social Isolation | Dr. K Interviews - YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bbQnnAbWBfE)

Loneliness is not just about lacking social connections, but also feeling emotionally misunderstood and disconnected from others.

---

Showing a mask to others in order to be accepted reinforces the idea that the only thing worth loving about oneself is the facade, leading to increased emotional loneliness.

###### When you meet someone, do you show them a mask? What makes it difficult to not put on a mask? What would happen if you didn’t put on a mask? What would it mean to you to be accepted or rejected by the person? What makes their reaction important to you?

[No One Will Ever Love Me](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7CceEyd6AwY)

Grounding yourself in experience can help stop the fluctuations of the mind and bring peace. Our minds and reality are fundamentally different, and conflating the two can lead to significant suffering.

---

The closer you examine your feelings of unlovability, the more they disappear, revealing that they are merely illusions.

###### When you feel that other people are thinking negatively of you, where does that feeling come from? Try to sit with the feeling and zoom out. Feel around it, if possible. Where does it start, where does it end? Become curious about it. You don’t have to answer the question, just use these questions as a means to develop awareness. In fact, be skeptical of the answers your mind comes up with. Write about your experience.
