Write down anything that's on your mind. Get it on paper. You need a place to put things.
Can you dump or delegate it? Break things into pieces & write due dates.

**The Distraction Resolution Process**
DI (distraction - dump; not important), DE (delegate), DO (do), DA (date)

Anything that isn't put into appropriate time & date will be stored in the mind & will distract you.

Most distractions are emotional distractions, not just activity distractions.

Everyone has a set of priorities in life - a set of values.
Things that support our values we label good & are attracted to.
Things that challenge our values we label bad & avoid. We want to not be distracted by these.
Your perceptions are: reality filtered by your values.
Anything we infatuate with, we're conscious of the upsides & unconscious of the downsides of.
Infatuations vie for our attention, occupy space & time, & run our life.
