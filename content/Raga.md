The Sanskrit word for the experience of attraction or likes. Raga means "attraction".

Attachment. Desire. Terrible emotions. Bad [[Vritti]].

I've also seen it translated as "color".

The result of not recognizing the difference between the changing material world and the permanent, [[Avidya]], finds its conclusion in [[Asmita]], one that is constantly reaffirmed and asserted though Raga/[[Dvesha]]. For, the actions we take as separate selves are not for the sake of desire or aversion themselves (as we assume), rather, we use our apparent preferences to build up a picture of ourselves in the material world.

Getting rid of Raga is called [[Vairagya]].
