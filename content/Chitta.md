Alias: unconscious, unconscious mind.

(Chit-tuh)

Focused attention.

The function through which you acquire knowledge. The reservoir of knowledge and memory, of merits and demerits, It contains the power of becoming from within, as well as the accumulation of knowledge from without. The intuitive library within you.

Means, "the **screen** of the mind". It gets translated into "the **unconscious** mind", but it isn't really unconscious. Literally the translation is "the **backdrop** of the mind". **Reservoir** of **impressions** and **memories**.
- *It's like the sky. The sky isn't really an object. It's the place where the sun, and the moon, and stars will appear, at times. Citta is the backdrop of our mind, and at any given moment, maybe the [[manas]] is active. Maybe the [[Buddhi]] is active. Maybe our [[Ego|ahamkara]] is active.*

[[Samskara|Samskara]]s are stored in the chitta and then sometimes become active.

The chitta is the screen on which all of the other [[Mind#The 5 parts of the mind]] "play".
