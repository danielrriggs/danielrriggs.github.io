The Basics
- Avoid Blue Light around sundown
	- Blue Block Glasses
	- Night mode for phone, Blue blocker on pc
		- Could be quackery / placebo, who knows - your call.
	- Limit / avoid screens
	- **Red light** the entire house (+ Google Home or phone voice assistance convenience - "Hey google, make my lights red") at sundown.
- Avoid / reduce stimulation - mental. Moderate physical stimulation.
	- Avoid media - YouTube, Games, arguments, podcasts. Everything.
		- In evening, but also during the day - reduce total cognitive load. Spend more time in silence. Frequently seek to experience a tolerable amount of suffering from boredom.
	- Turn greyscale mode on on your phone. Make it boring to use.
		- Mucking up your dopamine will muck up your sleep.
	- Practice Semen Retention - probably the **single most powerful** behavioral habit for mental health
- Sunlight
	- Exposure to AM sun exposure is the **most important** variable in stabilizing circadian rhythm. **Catch the rising sun** like your **life depends upon it**, because it does. Take this seriously.
		- Visualize misery of insomnia, depression, anxiety, etc to understand its importance.
	- PM sunlight can be good
	- Exposure to moonlight is good
- **Coffee** avoid after 2pm; preverably after 12; preferably none or minimal
	- When tempted to consume caffeine, visualize the misery of insomnia. Understand that the future misery will be worse.
	- I **half-strength** my coffee on my coffee machine.
- Exercise
	- Post-work out insomnia is very real
	- For advanced athletes, **Overtraining is a massive issue & easy to do!!!**
- Eat the right amount, at the right time
	- Don't go to be too hungry
	- Don't go to be with too much food.
	- If I wake up in middle of night, I eat a meal, then attempt to sleep again. If I lie awake for ~30 minutes, I get up, get coffee, and begin my day.
		- Understand that your performance will be inhibited today.
		- Understand that your psychology will be fragile today.
			- Set reasonable goals for today. Set your sights on progress. Don't let perfect be the enemy of the better, today.
			- Visualize the misery of insomnia to regulate mood & expectations.
- Exhaustion - physically and mentally expend yourself during the day. Win the night by winning the day.
- Sex & Semen Retention are significant factors. Much could be said on this topic, but pay attention to how these affect you.
- Sleep less if needed:
	- Improves likelihood of falling asleep the next day. Apply sparingly and time right. If I can't sleep, I get up and work.
		- Go to bed later if needed. Try to sleep again in a few hours.
- Leave phone across the room
	- Good habit to train your subconscious.
- Journal
	- Dump your thoughts
	- Plan the next day
- Absence of light and noise - earplugs + sleep mask.
	- Black out curtains
	- Black Electrical tape over any lights.
		- Feng Shui would recommend removing electronics from bedroom & including things like plants, arrange room properly to not stagnate qi, etc. Something to look into if you want.
- Temperature Control
	- Heater in winter, Window AC for bedroom.
- Silence - thoughts carry an energy. If the mind is always busy, those energies will persist. Venting a trauma can diminish the energy of the trauma (as long as it diminishes & processes rather than trigger a chain of more thoughts). Do nothing & let your brain think at you before you try to go to bed.
