[[Karma]] is a mystical thing as most people see it - "karma determines our destiny", "what goes around comes around", some people blame their karma for their circumstances.

[[Karma]] is just the principle of cause and effect. All effects are are preceded by causes and all causes lead to effects. This isn't something that is bizarre or mystical or spiritual or confusing.

On the one hand, we know that our actions have consequences. If I study very hard for a test, then the likelihood that I get an A goes up. If I study very hard for four years in school, then my GPA will be very good which sets me up for advantages if I'm applying to graduate school or med school or jobs or whatever.

What about the more abstract stuff? Is it karmic that I met my current partner? Is it karmic that I gave birth to a boy or a girl or even a special needs child? What happens to all of the different abstract parts of my life? Are those governed by cause and effect too? Because it's hard to see the cause and effect of why certain things happen in your life.

One example of karma that used to seem mystical was that some of the things about your life right now were even determined before you were born. Then Gregor Mendel came along and discovered genetics. Your height, eye color, hair color, certain aspects of your [[Personality]] can be tracked to genes.

[[Trauma]] is some kind of karmic action, something happened to you that then creates certain kinds of effects later in your life. [[Attachment Theory]] shows us that the way you were raised will determine to a certain degree how you form relationships in the future. does this mean that your relationships are destined? No.

You can make changes today.

We've even figured out in psychiatry what are the changes that you need to make to move from an anxious attachment to a secure attachment. We've even figured out what you need to do if your physiology is hyper aroused to recalibrate your physiology to a healthier level.

Karma isn't mystical, it just becomes difficult when we abstract things out.

Does the principle of cause and effect stop at some point?

What about other things like who you fall in love with or whether you get robbed or whether your car gets broken into, whether an accident happens when you're walking down the street? What are the causes for these things? This is where karma gets kind of hard to understand.

Thousands of years ago, people figured out that causes lead to effects and that this affects every dimension of our lives. Before we understood genetics or physics. We don't have a clear scientific explanation for the abstract aspects of our lives.
