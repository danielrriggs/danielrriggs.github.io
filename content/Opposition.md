Alias: Opponent Processing, Oppositional Framing, Opponent

###### The Opponent Processing Misnomer
Opponent processing doesn't exist, not because processing doesn't have conflict, but processing leads to cooperation. Your brain isn't in opponent processing with the rest of your brain. If it's opponent processing, you're both on a plane fighting over that plane. One of you has to win & the other has to lose.

We can look up & when we look up, we don't have to fight. Then we can cooperate. There can be friction and conflict, but we're using it to move up instead of fighting over the same flat two-dimensional ground.
