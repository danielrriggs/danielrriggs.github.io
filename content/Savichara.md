Relates to [[subtle|Subtle]] objects, after the [[gross]] have been left behind.

The subtleties of matter, energy, senses, and the mind are, themselves, the objects of meditation, inquiry, and [[Aparigraha|non-attachment]].

Subtle objects are either the building blocks of the gross objects, or are the mental instruments of cognition.

For example, all of the objects with shape and form are built of [[Prithvi|Earth]], [[Jal|Water]], [[Agni|Fire]], [[Vaayu|Air]], and [[Akasha|Space]] (bhutas are gross; _tattvas_ are subtle). All of the perceptions and attitudes are experienced by the sensing instruments, which now are themselves objects of meditation and inquiry.
