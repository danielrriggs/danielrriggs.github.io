Secure Sockets Layer, used to encrypt connections. Used by [[HTTPS]].

Typically, when the industry talks about SSL, we're talking about SSL 3.0. SSL 3.0 is now deprecated and has been superseded by [[TLS]].

SSL & TLS are both represented by the padlock icon in your browser.

SSL / TLS "certificate" is synonymous. These terms are often used interchangeably.

An SSL cert is used to authenticate the identity of a website.
