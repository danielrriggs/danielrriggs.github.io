Alias: Subtle Body, Mental Body, Astral Body, Psychological Body, Energy Body.

Responsible for emotion & thinking.

When the [[Sthula Sarira|Physical Body]] [[Sleep]]s, the astral body is still active.

###### Astral Body Constituents
The astral body consists of 19 constituents (there are differing views - some define it as 17):
- [[Indriya#The five Jnana Indriyas]]
- [[Indriya#The five Karma Indriyas]]
- [[Prana#Five Pranas (Vayus)]]
- Sense-consciousness
	- [[Manas]] (Memory)
	- [[Buddhi]] (Intellect)
	- [[Chitta]] ([[Mind]])
	- [[Ego|ahamkara]] (Ego)

The astral body contains the [[Pranamaya Kosha]], [[Manomaya Kosha]], and [[Vijnanamaya Kosha]].
