*Paramatman* is the universal or eternal Soul. It is one of two types of souls — the other being [[Jiva-atman]], which is the individual soul or self.

The term comes from the Sanskrit [[Parama]], meaning “highest” or “supreme,” and [[Atman]], meaning “self,” “soul” or “individual spirit.” In English, it is roughly translated to mean “primordial self” or "the self beyond."
