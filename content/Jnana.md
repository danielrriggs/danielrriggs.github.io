Alias: understanding, understand, wisdom, understood, Pragya, Prajna, knowledge.
// Knowledge is an alias for both VIdya & Jnana.

As opposed to [[Vidya]].

(Gyaan/Nyaan)

Subjective knowledge, or, understanding. Must be experienced. Wisdom. Transcendental knowledge.

Understanding isn't **transmissible**. My understanding of how to make good food could be different than yours.

Knowledge becomes understanding when you can actually implement it subconsciously.

---
“Knowledge,” in yogic terms, always carries a sense of distinguishing the real from the unreal.
