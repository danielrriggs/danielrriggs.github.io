Alias: Param Brahman

the "Supreme Brahman" and is beyond all descriptions and conceptualizations. It is described as a formlessness that pervades everything in the universe and beyond, eternally. Parabrahman is independent, has divine power over all, and is the creator, sustainer, and destroyer. Parabrahman is also the only controller of infinite universes and is above maya, which he controls with his power.

In Parabrahman is the seed of [[Sarvajna]].
