The internal instrument of the [[Mind]].

Consists of:
- [[Manas]]
- [[Buddhi]]
- [[Chitta]], which includes [[Samskara]]s
- [[Ego|ahamkara]]

The Antahkarana is like a wheel with many spokes, [[Karana]] means "A function taking place," and [[Antah]] means "inside". So, Antahkarana means your "internal functioning", or what is happening within you.
