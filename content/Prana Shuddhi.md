Prerequisites:
- Practice [[Nadi Shuddhi]] for 2-4 weeks.
- Practice [[Anuloma Viloma]] for 2-4 weeks.

This is an important practice for strengthening our [[Ajna Chakra]], as well as cleansing out our [[Prana]]. This requires you to be well grounded in [[Anuloma Viloma]].

We're going to follow our breath almost like an inverted V, as it goes up both of our nostrils and into this eyebrow center; a couple centimeters above where our eyebrows meet. So if you actually pay attention to the temperature of your breath, the feel of your breath, you'll notice that the breath actually converges right above your eyebrows.
- If you've practiced [[Anuloma Viloma]] well, and [[Nadi Shuddhi]] well, this should be something you're able to detect. If you're not really able to detect it, maybe this technique isn't really for you, or continue practicing [[Nadi Shuddhi]] in [[Anuloma Viloma]].
Essentially what we're going to do is breathe in into the eyebrow center, and then breathe out from the eyebrow center.
As you breathe in, imagine that inverted V going up between your eyebrows. And then breathe out and let it out from your eyebrows out into the world.
