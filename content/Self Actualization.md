Term from humanist psychologist Abraham Maslow. It means humans can control themselves. So that they can behave in a more ideal fashion. Whatever they've idealized about themselves. Then they'll be happy. That's the theory.

We were taught this, but it's wrong. Alot of actualized people don't have good health, have mental problems like anxiety, and kill themselves.

All self actualized people face meaninglessness.

Yoga says the only thing that is a problem is your own thought. Nothing else. Yoga's Self Actualization is known as [[Samadhi]]. It's reaching an ideal state, but it's a different ideal.
