A-U-M

There are 3 worlds the [[Mind]] can occupy at any given time.

A - the world of the senses.
U - the inner world. The world of **imagination**. [[Pratyahara]]. You don't live there 24/7. You go there, get some insights, then come back and talk to your friends.
M - [[Jnana|Pragya]]. The knowledge world.

[[Yoga]] is like Aum. It's all 3 worlds.

Without the knowledge world, we cannot make sense of the imaginary.
