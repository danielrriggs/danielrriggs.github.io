Alias: Spiritual

Most people think that the quest of spirituality begins with the question, who am I? This is a useless question. What's the confusion here? Don't you know who you are? 

You're supposed to know who you are. You're your father's son, your mother's son. It's who you are. You have history in this world and you also have a past life history about who you were in your past life. That's who you are.

The real spiritual questions are, what next? That's a real spiritual question. What next? Who do I want that next to it? How will we all benefit from it? How will we all profit from it? How can you have fun doing it? How can we grow our status by doing it?

Real spiritual quest is getting people together and growing your status together. Genuinely winning the respect of the whole population.

All immortals are going to be really energetic, really enthusiastic, happy people. Problems never end. The higher you increase your status, the bigger problems you're going to get. Somebody with a high status enjoys problems. They enjoy solving problems.

 If you want to know how high is your status, whether you're in a low status or high status, check this. How do you react to your problems? That shows your status right away. And how big of a problem do you have? What are the things that actually bother you? That shows your status.
