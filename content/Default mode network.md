The part of the brain that stitches together our life experiences and memories and gives us our sense of [[Nature of Self|self]] - the conclusions we've formed about ourselves. Active when we are thinking about ourselves. Where our sense of narrative and [[Ego|identity]] comes from.

The part of the brain that is occupied with "you".

---
A large-scale brain network primarily composed of the medial prefrontal cortex, posterior cingulate cortex/precuneus and angular gyrus.

It is best known for being active when a person is not focused on the outside world and the brain is at wakeful rest, such as during daydreaming and mind-wandering. It can also be active during detailed thoughts related to external task performance.
