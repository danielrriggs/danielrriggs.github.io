Our reaction to the environment creates stress, not the environment. Stress comes from conflicts. Resolve stress by making a decision.

Your toxic job isn't creating your stress, it's your indecision about whether to not speak up to change it or to leave it or to stay and tolerate it.

You are responsible for manufacturing [[Stress Physiology]] chemicals. Take responsibility for it. You must practice feeling great to feel great, otherwise you'll be miserable. See [[The Art of Joyful Living]].

When you practice [[Pratyahara]], conflicts will come up. There's a process called [[Samskarabhisarjuna]]. Invest 10 to 15 minutes a day for 30 days.

Every thought comes with presuppositions, which determine your experience. Calculate how much money are you losing every month because of stress - we're using $ so we can put a quantity on it. If it's a small amount of $, then just continue to be stressed. If it's a large amount of $, then get serious about your resolutions.
