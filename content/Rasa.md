Alias: Rasa Theory.

Emotion, mood, feeling. Essence.

Rasa is like Aristotle's [[Catharsis]].

From the [[Bharata Natyashastra]].
###### 9 Rasas:
- joy (hasya)
- fear (bhayanaka)
- anger (raudra)
- love ([[Sringara]]) (eroticism) | light green
- courage (vira)
- sadness (karuna)
- amazement (adbhuta)
- disgust (vibhatsya)
- calmness (shanti).
Each Rasa is a divinity, a god.

[[Bhava]] - 49 total
sthayi Bhava - primary emotions; dominant states - 8 types
vyabhicari Bhava - transitory emotions; temperamental states - 33 types
sattvika Bhava - emotions coming together; involuntary emotions. Occur at subconscious level. Out of our control. - 8 types
