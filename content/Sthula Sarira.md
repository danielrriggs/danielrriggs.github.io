Alias: Physical Body, Gross Body.

Also called the gross body, this body includes our muscles, bones, organs, and all the other parts that make up your physical form.

The gross body is made up of the [[Panchabhuta|Five Primary Elements]].

[[Ayurveda]] is based on the principle that these five elements must be regularly refreshed to keep the body healthy and free from disease.

The physical body is the tool (like a pen). The pen does not write; we write with with the use of the pen.

The physical body contains the [[Annamaya Kosha]].

###### Nine Gates
The physical body is considered to be a town having nine gates:
- 2 eyes
- 2 ears
- 2 nostrils
- 1 mouth
- 1 excretory organ (anus or rectum)
- 1 regenerative organ (genitals)
