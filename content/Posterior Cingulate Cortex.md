Alias: PCC

Closer to the front of our brain.

Tells you where you are. It's a combination of a sense of [[Ego|identity]] along with like an integration of your current circumstances. The part of your brain that organizes all of the inputs that you have. But, not just sensory input. It also adds meaning and planning and action. When it's functioning well, you can manage all of the stuff around you.

*Example: you miss a flight. You need to figure out where you are and integrate lots of information around you. How do I find a new flight? Who else do I need to call? Do I need to make sure that my bags that got checked in like end up in the right place?*

This is a higher order part of the brain.
