Summary of what we learned:

- Trauma is not a malfunction, it's an adaptation.
As we go through life, experiences teach us lessons which helped us survive yesterday, but create problems for today. Our goal is to rewire ourselves to be adaptive to the life we live in today and create the life you want to live tomorrow.

- Physiology
	- [[Physiologic Rewiring]]
	- [[Somatic Illness]]
This begins with reconnecting with your body. The body becomes a source of pain and something to be avoided. Do Mind-body practices like [[Yoga]] or [[Tai Chi]].

- [[Emotion]]s
The next thing that we need to do is become comfortable with our emotions. In [[Trauma]], we experience [[Hemispheric Lateralization]] where we numb ourselves to our emotional experience.

Growing up in environments that are abusive, controlling, or neglectful, our emotions don't accomplish what they're supposed to (emotions are sources of information and [[Motivation]]). When my emotion can't motivate me or create the right kind of situation around me, then the only choice that I have is to numb myself from it, because it's a constant source of pain. But as we numb ourselves to our emotions, they start to act in the background. They start to affect our relationships in stealthy ways and can manifest as things like [[Addiction]] because we have a lot of pain. We have all of these subsurface emotions that we literally need a psychoactive substance to keep down there.

Once we develop a healthy relationship with our emotions, we can then move on to some of the cognitive manifestations of trauma.

- Cognitive Manifestations of Trauma
These are things like the paralysis of initiation, where for some reason it feels like I just can't get started.
[[Causes of the Paralysis of Initiation]]
[[Overcoming the Paralysis of Initiation]]
This can also be things like very [[Impulsivity|impulsive]] behavior, where even though on some level you want to move towards a [[Goal]], your [[Mind]] wakes up one day and you decide, that you're going to abandon that [[Goal]] and do something else.

When people grow up with a history of [[Trauma]] or have traumatic experiences later in life, something about the compass that they have in life gets disturbed. You can't consistently move towards a particular direction. Sometimes it feels like you're living your life on autopilot, or that your life is happening all around you with no ability to [[Self-Control|control]] it. As we explore those issues and engage in some of these practices, hopefully we can start to gain some [[Self-Control|control]] and direction in our life.

- [[Identity (trauma)|Identity]]
The next manifestation that we want to tackle is our very sense of [[Identity (trauma)|Identity]], because [[Trauma]] shapes our [[Perception]] of ourself. Once we [[Perception|perceive]] ourself to be a certain way, this can become a self-fulfilling prophecy. Once I believe that I'm not worthy of love, my experience in relationships will be very, very different. I'll allow people to walk all over me. Any person who gives me any amount of attention is something that I will be grateful for instead of really understanding what I truly deserve. [[Trauma]] can drastically shape the way that our mind forms connections with others.

Exploring [[Attachment]] and [[Attachment Theory]] gives us insight into some of our [[Behavioral reinforcement|behavior]]s and how to fix them.

- Forming a [[Narrative]]
The basic problem in [[Trauma]] is once we disconnect from our emotions and once we develop an [[Identity (trauma)|Identity]], we become stuck in time; in this mode of living in our [[Trauma]]; this can manifest as things like flashbacks. The [[Trauma]] defines us in the present and even seems to create a destiny for us in the future. Creating a [[Narrative]], literally putting the [[Trauma]] behind us, allows us to move past it and start to plan towards a different future.

- [[Spirituality|Spiritual]] perspectives
Having some understanding of [[karma]], the [[Nature of Self]], has helped many people move forward. It helps people understand the rules of life from a different perspective.
